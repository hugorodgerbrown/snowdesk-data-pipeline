# Snowdesk — DESIGN.md

This file condenses the [Snowdesk Design System](https://claude.ai/artifact/6n3bKzXkQfr5QsANFDjPUq)
into the DESIGN.md format for coding agents and design tools. It was
generated from that system's `tokens.json`, synced from `main@40a5bb7` on
25 September 2026. It is a copy, not a source: token values live in the
`@theme` block of [`src/css/main.css`](src/css/main.css), and when the two
disagree the CSS wins. Regenerate this file from the design system after a
token change rather than editing it by hand.

The `map-*` colours are not `@theme` tokens. They name the custom
properties declared on the map page in
[`static/css/map.css`](static/css/map.css) (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--muted`,
…) and the literal paint colours in [`static/js/map.js`](static/js/map.js).
`mark-pine` is the logo colour from [`static/favicon.svg`](static/favicon.svg).

## Overview

Snowdesk shows the avalanche bulletin as the provider published it — every field, verifiable against the source — and adds what the provider cannot: finer grain, field observations, routes, offline maps. **The map is the primary surface**: a danger choropleth over a basemap, the reader's own places and routes on top, glass controls at the edges. The bulletin page behind it reads like a well-edited publication, not a dashboard. Calm, confident, quietly expert; British English; no emoji; never tell the reader to go or stay.

## Colors

- **Ground and ink:** `bg` #f2f0ec page, `card` #ffffff surfaces, `border` rgba(0,0,0,.09) hairlines. Text `text-1` #1a1916 (headings), `text-2` #6b6860 (body), `text-3` #6e6b65 (meta — AA boundary; never dim further).
- **EAWS danger scale — a regulated standard, not a palette:** `eaws-low` #ccff66, `eaws-moderate` #ffff00, `eaws-considerable` #ff9900, `eaws-high` #ff0000, `eaws-very-high` #820100. Theme-invariant; never re-tint or reuse. Ink on the fill is `eaws-*-fg` (near-black 1–3, white 4–5). Pale bands use `eaws-*-tint` with `eaws-*-text`.
- **Status** pairs (`status-*-bg` / `status-*-text`) only by meaning. **Offline availability** is its own family: `sync-ok`, `sync-off`, `sync-partial`, `sync-blocked`.
- **Accent** `accent` #2563eb is an interactive blue with no severity: switch ON, the "overlay shown" ring. Not decoration.
- **Map hues are owned:** EAWS yellow→red; favourites `marker-favourite` #1a73e8; observations `marker-observation` #e8711a; routes `route-line` #c026d3 (legs: climb `route-rail-climb` #64748b dashed, descent `route-rail-descent` #c026d3); pending share `route-line-pending` #0d9488; basemap identity colours per basemap. A new mark takes a hue nobody owns.

## Typography

- **DM Sans** 400–600 for everything read; **DM Mono** 400/500 for figures, metadata, eyebrows and chips. No serif yet (the map popup title uses Georgia).
- `page-title` 24/32 semibold, tracking −0.025em. `prose` 14/1.6 for provider text. `eyebrow` mono 12 uppercase 0.1em in `text-3`. `pill` mono 9.5 uppercase for chips. `row-meta` mono 12 ("12.9 km · 497 m asc · 4h15m").
- Map chrome labels are mono: readout 13 semibold, date 11 semibold at 0.06em.

## Layout

- Tailwind 4px ramp. Bulletin and sheets in a 640px column; legal 720; grids to 1200. Breakpoints 600 and 960.
- **The map fills the viewport.** Floating chrome sits 12px (+ safe area) from every edge: readout chip, pin and download top-left; search top-right; the control column bottom-right (gap 8); zoom, help, legend, date and season scrubber bottom-left. Sheets dock to the same insets so their edges line up with the roundels.
- While a route is open, the rails dock at the foot and the other chrome fades out.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is **glass, not shadow**. Floating controls: `glass` (rgba(255,255,255,.85) light / rgba(20,22,26,.78) dark) + backdrop blur 8px + 0.5px border + `0 2px 14px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`. Popups are more translucent (`popup-glass`, blur 14) so the choropleth shows through. Cards use a hairline border and at most a small shadow. Stacking: banner 40 < toast 50 < sheet 60 < modal 70 < launch 80.

## Shapes

`card` 12px (cards, sheets, popups, panels), `tag` 8px (tags, menu items, legend), `sm` 6px, `pill` 4px (chips, badges). Every map control is fully round at an **even** height — 40 (floating alone), 32 (ribbon header, info row), 24 (inside the scrubber). Icons: 24×24 stroke partials, stroke 2, round caps, `currentColor`.

## Components

- **Button** (`_button.html`): 14px medium, `tag` radius, 20×10 padding (compact 16×8); primary `text-1` fill with `card` text, secondary 1px `text-1` outline, ghost `text-3`/30 outline, accent for saving somebody else's object; destructive only for the last, irreversible action.
- **Rating block**: EAWS band header (tint + text), aspect/elevation row, the provider's prose in full, field notes collapsed beneath a rule and credited to SLF.
- **Day-character callout**: Snowdesk's label and one-line summary, visibly distinct from the provider layer.
- **Collapsible panel**: how length is managed — disclosure, never omission.
- **Map roundel**: glass circle, 18px glyph; hover = `chip-strong` wash; overlay on = inset 2px accent ring; disabled dims only the glyph (.45).
- **Region readout chip → region panel**: mono name + EAWS swatch; opens into a glass panel with breadcrumb, bulletin CTA, resorts, pinned regions.
- **Route rails**: rail one = identity + a profile with one fill per leg (climb slate, descent fuchsia); rail two = the pressed leg's terrain.
- **UGC panel rows / overflow menu**: label first; two or more actions collapse into "…" in the order Plan a trip → Share → Rename → Delete.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do render every field the provider published; manage length with disclosure.
- Do keep Snowdesk's own content visibly separate from the provider's and credit its source.
- Do use tokens: never a raw hex or Tailwind palette class in a template (SVG fill/stroke excepted); MapLibre literals name the token they mirror.
- Do give every map mark a halo or casing so it reads over every EAWS fill.
- Don't recommend behaviour — characterise and explain.
- Don't re-tint, reuse or theme the EAWS colours.
- Don't add accent colour or visual weight to make a feature feel important.
- Don't put two inline actions in a restricted area; collapse them into "…".
- Don't use an odd height on a round map control.
