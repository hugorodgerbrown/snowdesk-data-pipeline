Every row always renders — a blank field shows a dashed-box placeholder rather than being omitted, so missing curation stays visible: the public/non-staff placeholder is an em-dash, the staff placeholder (``is_staff``) is an explicit "Add " hint. 4. Either the favourite/unfavourite star (``can_favourite``) or a sign-in CTA. 5. "View resort →" CTA linking to the resort's own page (SNOW-504) — the bulletin link itself now lives on that page rather than in the popup.
