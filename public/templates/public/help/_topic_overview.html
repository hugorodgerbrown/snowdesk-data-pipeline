{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk brings together official avalanche bulletins from three providers — SLF (Switzerland), ALBINA (Austria/EUREGIO), and Météo-France — onto a single interactive map, with a consistent layout for every region and every day. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} You don't need an account to browse: pick a region on the map, read today's danger rating and avalanche problems, or scrub back through the season to see how conditions have evolved. Signing in unlocks extras such as email alerts and saved favourite locations. {% endblocktrans %}
