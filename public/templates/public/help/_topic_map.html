{% blocktrans trimmed %} The map colours every region by its current avalanche danger level, from pale green (Low) through to red (Very High). Use the search box to jump straight to a region by name, the locate-me button to centre the map on your position, and pinch or scroll to zoom and pan. The info panel in the corner shows the legend and the current data sources. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A basemap picker lets you switch between the standard map style and each country's national mapping-agency basemap (Swisstopo, IGN, basemap.at) where available. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} An overlay toggle shows your saved favourite pins directly on the map, alongside the resorts layer. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A community-reports overlay shows anonymised, publicly-shared field observations from other Snowdesk users on the map. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The Report button lets you submit a quick field observation — conditions you've seen in person — for the region you're viewing. {% endblocktrans %}
