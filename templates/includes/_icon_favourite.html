A star means "favourite" everywhere in this product, and the region pin control needs the same mark in both of its states — outline for "pin this", solid for "pinned, press to unpin" — which is the convention the star already carries everywhere else it is used as a toggle. Two glyphs for one meaning was what the region pin shipped with: a literal ``★``/``☆`` text character in the button and a trash can on the row that undid it, so the same act was drawn twice, differently, and one of the drawings said "delete".
