{% load i18n %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_route_point_card.html — the point header: one point on the open route, read in words (SNOW-1064; a header of the route panel since SNOW-1068; headings since SNOW-1069; the line labels since SNOW-1094). The route panel (_route_rail.html) is one card: the route's header, its profile, a hint, and this — the point under the hint at the panel's full width (2026-10-18; it replaced the route's title and meta line from SNOW-1068 until then). On a phone it is the route sheet's HALF state. It holds the aspect wheel (aspect_wheel_core.js, SNOW-1063) at 48 px and two labelled lines: SKIER'S TRACK, beside the wheel and centred on it — heading, how the track lies on the slope, and its step's band of degrees on its own scale (a climb's and a descent's bounds differ), "NW → W · Descent · 5–15°" — and TERRAIN SLOPE, under both at the card's full width (2026-10-19) — the ground in the EAWS words and its class's band ("Extremely steep · 40°+"), with the side the ground falls on a traverse or a turn, or both sides where a turn crosses the fall line ("…, falling skier's left, then right"). The heading names the sectors the wheel's inner ring lights; up or down is the wheel's centre mark. The degrees are the step's band, never the measured angle. The words come from route_point_card_core.js, and are also the wheel's accessible name, "Skier's track: …; Terrain slope: …", read with "to" for the arrow and commas for the dots. UNLIT until the route cursor has an index: the wheel drawn with nothing lit and a dash on each line, so a phone sheet pulled to HALF before a tap shows what a tap will fill (2026-10-18). static/js/route_point_card.js fills it; from sm up the rail hides it while no point is placed (static/css/map.css). THE WHEEL CLEARS THE POINT (2026-10-02): it is a button (`[data-route-point-card-clear]`), disabled with no point, and pressing it keeps the route open, as the first Escape does. The panel's × always closes the route. The strings template carries every word the header says, under ``{% trans %}``, so makemessages sees them and no literal reaches the DOM from JavaScript (i18n-lint). Context: static : bool, optional — render visible, for the component library, which runs no JS to reveal it. state_json, headline, ground : optional — a filled header for the component library: the wheel's state JSON-encoded and the two lines as route_point_card_core.js's ``reading`` would write them. {% endcomment %}
