{% load i18n %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_route_point_card.html — the point header: one point on the open route, read in words (SNOW-1064; a header of the route panel since SNOW-1068; headings since SNOW-1069; the Track and Slope labels since SNOW-1094). The route panel (_route_rail.html) is one card pinned top-left with the profile always showing and a header in one of two states: the route, or a point. This partial is the second. The rail includes it inside its identity block, under the eyebrow and the buttons' row so it has the panel's full width (SNOW-1069), and hides the title and meta line while a point is placed; the eyebrow becomes the route's name. Placing a point or opening a route changes only what the panel says, so the panel never moves under the finger. It holds the aspect wheel (aspect_wheel_core.js, SNOW-1063) at 48 px and two labelled lines: TRACK — heading, then the track's steepness on its own scale and how it lies on the slope as one phrase, "NW → W · Gentle descent" — and SLOPE, the ground in the EAWS words ("Extremely steep"), with the side the ground falls on a traverse or a turn, or both sides where a turn crosses the fall line ("…, falling skier's left, then right"). The heading names the sectors the wheel's inner ring lights; up or down is the wheel's centre mark. No degrees. The words come from route_point_card_core.js, and are also the wheel's accessible name, "Track: …; Slope: …", read with "to" for the arrow and commas for the dots. HIDDEN until the route cursor has an index; static/js/route_point_card.js shows it and fills it. THE WHEEL CLEARS THE POINT (2026-10-02): it is a button (`[data-route-point-card-clear]`), and pressing it keeps the route open, as the first Escape does. The panel's × always closes the route. The strings template carries every word the header says, under ``{% trans %}``, so makemessages sees them and no literal reaches the DOM from JavaScript (i18n-lint). Context: static : bool, optional — render visible, for the component library, which runs no JS to reveal it. state_json, headline, ground : optional — a filled header for the component library: the wheel's state JSON-encoded and the two lines as route_point_card_core.js's ``reading`` would write them. {% endcomment %}
