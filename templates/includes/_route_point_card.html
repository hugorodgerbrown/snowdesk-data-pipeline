{% load i18n components %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_route_point_card.html — the point card: one point on the open route, read in words (SNOW-1064). Pinned to the map's top-left corner while a route is open, under where the region chip sits, and never moved: each tap on the line, drag along the profile or mouse over the line changes only what it says, so it never ends up under the finger or over the line being read. It shares one left-aligned column with the route rail (400 px wide on desktop, the map's width less its insets on a phone — static/css/map.css). It holds the aspect wheel (aspect_wheel_core.js, SNOW-1063) at 48 px and two lines: the HEADLINE — the track's steepness on its own scale and how it crosses the slope ("Very steep fall line descent") — and the GROUND in the EAWS words ("Extremely steep slope"). No degrees, headings or aspects: the map shows direction. The words come from route_point_card_core.js, and are also the wheel's accessible name. EMPTY until the route cursor has an index: the wheel unlit and "Select a point on the route to view terrain data". It opens and closes with the rail. The × clears the point and keeps the route; so does the first Escape (static/js/route_rail.js).
