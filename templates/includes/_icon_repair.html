{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_repair.html — the "fetch what is missing again" glyph: a circular arrow over a downward stroke (SNOW-844). Used by the Manage downloads sheet's Repair control (includes/_map_downloads_row_actions.html), which refetches the style/sprite/TileJSON documents a downloaded area is missing rather than re-downloading its tiles. Deliberately NOT includes/_icon_download_arrow.html, which sits two controls away on the same row and means "fetch this area onto this device" — a whole download. A repair is the same verb applied to a hole in one, so it is the download arrow inside a refresh loop: recognisably related, unmistakably not the same action.
