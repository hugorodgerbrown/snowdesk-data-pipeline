{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_chevron.html — the expand glyph: a chevron pointing down (SNOW-711). One source for one mark, the same reason the pencil and the trash beside it are partials. Its caller is includes/_row_disclosure.html, the UGC row's trailing expand control. It points DOWN at rest and is rotated 180° by its own control when the row is expanded, so the mark always points at where the content will appear (or at the row it will fold back into). That is the meaning `includes/_collapsible_panel.html` and static/js/map_controls_collapse.js already give a chevron in this codebase: expand in place, not navigate away. An arrow would say the opposite — the "Details →" link this replaced said exactly that, and led somewhere.
