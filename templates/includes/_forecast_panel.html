{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_forecast_panel.html — multi-day outlook for one location. Two parts: 1. A compact day strip — one column per day: weekday label, condition icon, hi/lo temperature, that day's freezing level, and a snowfall chip when the day's ``snowfall_sum`` is non-zero. 2. Expandable hourly detail — one ``_collapsible_panel.html`` per day that carries an hourly series, listing it via ``_forecast_hourly_body.html``. SNOW-761: the days come from ONE ``Weather`` row — its own ``observed_on`` leads, and the rest are the entries in its ``forecast`` column (``build_point_forecast_panel``). Before the rebuild they were N sibling rows from a second table. **``hourly`` is present on only the first few forward days**, so part 2 tests each day for one rather than assuming; a day past that horizon simply contributes no panel. Consumers: the resort page (one strip per curated location) and the favourite detail card.
