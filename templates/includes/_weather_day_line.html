{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_weather_day_line.html — region 3 of the location forecast page (SNOW-789). The selected day, stated once: its date and condition, then the two figures that decide whether a plan holds — where it is freezing, and how long there is light to be out in. **High and low are deliberately absent.** They are in the cell the reader just pressed, directly above this line, and in the meteogram directly below it. Stating them a third time is the duplication this ticket removed. Freezing level is tested ``is not None`` rather than for truthiness: 0 m means freezing at the valley floor, which is a real reading on an avalanche page and not a blank to hide. **Visibility is a property of this root element, not of a wrapper.** The reveal rule resolves to ``display: flex``, and this row IS the flex container — put ``.forecast-day-line`` on a wrapper instead and the row becomes a lone flex item that shrinks to its content, taking the ``border-t`` with it. So ``reveal_index`` is passed in and lands here. Omit ``reveal_index`` and the row carries neither the hiding class nor the index, and simply renders. That is the backfilled one-day page: with no picker there is no radio, so no ``:has()`` rule could ever match and a row that opted into the hiding class would be hidden forever. Context: day : ForecastPanelDay — one entry from ``build_point_forecast_panel``'s ``days``. reveal_index : int — optional. This day's position in the picker. Set it and the row is hidden until that day's radio is checked; omit it and the row is always visible. testid_prefix : str — data-testid namespace.
