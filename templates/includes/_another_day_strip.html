Most people, most of the time, are on today, so a page or sheet showing any other day says so plainly and offers the way back. Rendered only when the day is not today — there is no hidden state — by: - the region and resort PAGES, full width directly under the nav, where "Back to today" is a link to the page's bare URL (the bare URL is today), so it works with JavaScript off; - the map's region and place SHEETS, at the top of the sheet above the peek row, where "Back to today" is a button carrying ``data-back-to-today``: static/js/map_today.js moves the scrubber to today rather than leaving the map. The sheet re-renders on every change of day, so the strip comes and goes with it. Status-info palette by meaning: it is information about what is on screen, not a warning, and the EAWS yellows and oranges must never be borrowed for anything that is not a danger level. Parameters (passed via {% include … with … %}): day — the date being shown (a date object). href — the page's bare URL. Renders a link; omit in a sheet. placement — "page" (default: full width, a rule beneath) or "sheet" (a rounded band inside the sheet's padding). {% endcomment %}
