{% comment %} templates/includes/_place_link_row.html — one row that names another kind of place and opens it: a resort's region, a region's resort, a weather point's region or resort (Hugo, 25 Oct 2026). THREE KINDS OF PLACE, ONE WAY ACROSS. The region, resort and weather sheets each answer one question — the region the avalanche danger, the resort the place and its weather, the weather point one spot at one height — and each ends with rows like this one that take the reader to its neighbours. The row leads with the tile of the kind it OPENS (the square for a region, the round cabin for a resort), so the reader can tell where a tap goes before making it. A region row carries no danger level by default: the level is the region's own panel's to state, and a resort has no danger of its own. The one exception is the resort body's bulletin callout (Hugo, 27 Oct 2026), which passes ``day_rating`` so the square shows the REGION's level for the day in place of the map glyph — the row still names the region and opens it, so the level reads as the region's. STILL A REAL LINK. ``href`` is the place's page, so the row works with no JavaScript and a modified click opens the page in a new tab. On the map, ``static/js/map_panel_trail.js`` reads ``data-panel-open`` and opens the place's sheet in place instead, with a back pill naming where the reader came from. A resort row also carries ``data-resort-slug``, the attribute the region sheet's own handler has opened resorts by since SNOW-1161. Context: kind — "region", "resort" or "weather": the kind of place it opens. title — the place's name. subtitle — one short line under it, or "". href — the place's page. open_id — what the map opens it by: a region id ("CH-4115"), a resort slug, or a location short id. testid — optional ``data-testid``. day_rating — optional, kind "region" only: a RegionDayRating whose ``max_rating`` the square shows as the EAWS danger tile. forecast_icon — optional: a weather icon filename (``weather_icon_filename``) drawn before the chevron, with ``forecast_temp`` beside it. The Today panel's resort rows (SNOW-1366) carry the resort's weather this way; no other caller passes it. forecast_temp — optional, with ``forecast_icon``: the day's maximum, °C. nested — optional: indent the row under the one above it, as the Today panel nests a region's resorts (SNOW-1366). announce_level — optional, with ``day_rating``: say the level to a screen reader ("Danger today: Considerable"), since the tile is aria-hidden.
