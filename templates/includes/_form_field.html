{% comment %} Form field primitive (SNOW-672). One labelled control with its error list — the shape every account form repeats: a spacing wrapper, the label, the widget Django renders, and the errors beneath it. Ten call sites across six templates had written it out by hand, identically, which is ten places for the label weight or the error colour to drift and nothing to notice when one of them does. The widget itself is not rendered here — Django renders it from the form, with the classes from ``{% input_classes %}`` (see apps/public/templatetags/components.py) applied in the form's widget attrs. This partial owns the chrome around it. Context: field — a bound form field (required). Supplies the widget, its label id and its errors. label — label copy (required).
