Connectivity symbol (SNOW-748): A [data-network-indicator] beside the sync badge, rendered for every viewer including anonymous ones and NEVER hidden — the phone-status-bar half of the aeroplane-mode model this feature follows. static/js/pwa_offline.js swaps its glyph, its colour and its accessible name between "using the network" and "not using the network", and writes the same bit to [data-network-state].
