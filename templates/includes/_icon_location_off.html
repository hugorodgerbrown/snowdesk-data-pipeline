{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_location_off.html — the "we cannot place you" glyph: a map pin struck through (SNOW-869). Its caller is includes/_overlay_banner.html's "location-off" icon variant, the roundel on the off-map nudge — the banner shown when a located position falls outside every mapped region. Feather's `map-pin-off`: the pin is drawn as four arcs rather than one closed outline so the strike-through line passes between them instead of crossing a stroke, which is what keeps the mark legible at 18px. Editing it means editing all five subpaths together, so it belongs behind one partial — docs/decisions/ui-icons-are-house-stroke-partials.md. Note the sibling glyph. includes/_icon_locate.html is the *working* locate control; this is the same subject in its failed state. They are deliberately different marks rather than one mark with a modifier, because they appear on different surfaces and never side by side.
