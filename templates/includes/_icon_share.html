{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_share.html — the share glyph: a node passing to two others (SNOW-764). One source for one mark, the same reason _icon_pencil.html and _icon_trash.html beside it are partials. Its caller is the route row's action cluster (routes/partials/_route_row_actions.html), where it sits first, before Rename and the always-last trash. WHY THE THREE-NODE GRAPH rather than the iOS box-and-arrow. The box-and-up-arrow is a platform mark, and on Android it reads as "upload" or "open in"; the three linked nodes is the mark Android itself uses and is the one a cross-platform web surface can carry without claiming to be either platform. What the control does is hand this route to another PERSON, and a graph of people is closer to that than an arrow leaving a box. STYLE — a 24-grid 2px stroke, matching _icon_pencil.html's pencil and _icon_route.html's track. The three nodes are filled circles rather than outlines because at 17px an outlined 2.5px circle is a smudge; the two connecting strokes carry the stroke width instead. Drawn for this ticket, so there is no third-party attribution to carry. Parameters (passed via {% include … with … %}): size (int, optional) — rendered px, both axes.
