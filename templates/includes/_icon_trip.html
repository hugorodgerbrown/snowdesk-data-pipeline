{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_trip.html — the trip glyph: a map pin (SNOW-830). One source for one mark. Its caller is the route row's overflow menu (routes/partials/_route_row_menu_items.html), where it labels "Plan a trip" — the item that leaves the panel for apps/trips/views.py's authoring page. NAMED FOR THE DOMAIN, NOT THE SHAPE, exactly as _icon_route.html is: a trips surface of its own will want the same mark, and a partial called ``_icon_pin`` would either be reused for map pins that mean something else or duplicated. What the glyph stands for here is a trip, not a pin. WHY A PIN. A trip is a route plus a meeting point at a stated time (apps/trips/models.py) — the meeting point is the thing a trip adds to a route, and the pin is the mark for "we meet here". A calendar day would name the other half, but the route row already sits in a list of routes, so the half worth drawing is the one the route does not have. STYLE — a 24-grid 2px stroke, matching _icon_route.html's track and _icon_share.html's graph. Menu items render it at 17px, the row-action glyph size. Drawn for this ticket, so there is no third-party attribution to carry. Parameters (passed via {% include … with … %}): size (int, optional) — rendered px, both axes.
