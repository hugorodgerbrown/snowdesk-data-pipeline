{% load i18n components %} {% comment %} includes/_route_rail.html — the route panel: the open route's profile, cut at its transitions, under a header for the route or a point (SNOW-1018, restacked by SNOW-1065, one panel since SNOW-1068, points only since 2026-10-02). A RAIL is the card pinned top-left while a route is open (docs/glossary.md). There is one: SNOW-1065 retired rail two. Top to bottom it reads: the HEADER — the route's NAME as the title and its META LINE as the subtitle, the routes list's own format and strings ("12.9km · 337m ↑ · 1906m ↓ · 2h51m", routes/partials/_route.html), the time only when the recording has one; the LANE — the profile, below; the HINT — "Tap or drag the profile to view terrain data", until a point is placed; the POINT HEADER — the aspect wheel and the reading (_route_point_card.html) for the placed point. Pressing the wheel clears the point. ON A PHONE THE THREE SHEET STATES ARE THREE DEPTHS of that list (2026-10-18; re-cut 2026-10-31, Hugo): PEEK, the minimised state, ends under the meta line, so the route stays open with its title and figures and the map gets the rest; HALF, the state a tap on a route opens, adds the claim, the profile, the hint and the point header, unlit until a point is placed (a point placed at peek raises the sheet to it); FULL adds the TERRAIN DATA, every figure behind the point, and only the terrain slope line opens it: a tap on the panel or a drag steps between PEEK and HALF (data-detents-reserved). From sm up the panel has no detents: the point header shows while a point is placed, and the terrain data while it is open. THE TERRAIN DATA IS EVERYONE'S, AND HIDDEN UNTIL ASKED FOR (SNOW-1146). It was a staff debug readout (SNOW-1099). A press on the point header's TERRAIN SLOPE line (`[data-route-terrain-toggle]`) reveals it: on a phone by raising the sheet to FULL, which nothing else reaches; from sm up by opening it in the card (`data-route-terrain-open`). The figures come from the owner's own terrain rows (routes:terrain); a pending share has no uuid and so no rows, and shows no toggle. Then the LANE, the elevation profile drawn as one filled shape per leg under one outline, with distance ticks along its foot. 400 px wide on desktop, the map's width less its insets on a phone (`--route-column-width`, static/css/map.css), where a 13 km profile's shape reads rather than drawing near flat across a whole desktop. A TAP ON THE PROFILE'S TOP LINE places the point at that distance, with a generous margin either side of the line but not down in the fill; a tap on the route's line on the map places it too. The point is drawn on the lane as a vertical line with a dot where it crosses the outline, the point's ELEVATION at the top of the line and its DISTANCE from the start at the bottom (`[data-route-rail-readout]`), right of the line unless they would not fit. A sideways drag from anywhere on the profile scrubs the point (restored 2026-10-19). The legs are drawn and never pressed: leg selection was removed on 2026-10-02. RENDERED ONCE, EMPTY, AND HIDDEN. static/js/route_rail.js fills it for the route map.js's `activateRoute` opens and hides it again when the route detail sheet closes. Nothing here depends on which route that is, so the partial carries no route context at all — only the URL templates the script addresses the open route's endpoints with. INSIDE #map, NOT AFTER IT. The page body is a flex column and #map is its growing child, so a sibling after it would take height from the map and resize the canvas on every open. Pinned top-left instead — under the region chip's row on desktop, on the top edge on a phone, where that row is withdrawn while a route is open — it moves nothing else on the map: opening a route or placing a point changes only what the panel says (static/css/map.css). Inside #map also keeps a press on the rail from counting as a click outside an open map sheet (static/js/map_sheet.js excludes #map). THE ACTIONS ARE A MENU. Four actions in a restricted area collapse into the "…" menu (design-system rule 5): the routes row's own four in its order — includes/_route_rail_menu_items.html. All four are owner actions, so a pending share has no menu. A Terrain item led them and opened the route detail sheet until SNOW-1095 removed both. ITS OWN CLOSE, BECAUSE ITS LIFETIME IS ITS OWN. The rail opens on a tap and is not a map sheet, so it cannot borrow a sheet's dismissal. The × beside the menu is the one control for it, the sheet header's × drawn the same way, and it always closes the route: the point header's wheel is what clears a point. It is not a second action competing for rule 5's threshold: it dismisses the surface rather than acting on the route, the same place every map sheet keeps its ×. A PENDING SHARE'S SAVE SITS HERE. `[data-route-rail-claim]` holds the "Save route" / "Sign in to save this route" control map.js builds, shown for a pending share only: a recipient lands on the rail, and a Save one menu press away is a Save most recipients would never find. TICK LABELS AND THE POINT'S READOUT ARE HTML, NOT SVG. The lane is stretched to the rail's width with `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, and SVG text and circles scale with its viewBox, so a label or a dot drawn inside it would be squashed or stretched with the strip. The tick labels sit in `[data-route-rail-ticks]` under it and the readout over it, placed by percentage.
