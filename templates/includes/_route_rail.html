A RAIL is the strip below the map while a route is open (docs/glossary.md). Rail one is the whole route. Three columns: the identity block (the eyebrow, the route's name, its figures and its actions), the LANE — the elevation profile drawn as one filled shape per leg under one outline, with distance ticks along its foot — and a readout column naming the open leg. Pressing a leg publishes it to the route cursor (static/js/route_cursor_core.js) as the open leg; rail two (SNOW-1017) renders it. Pressing it again closes it. There is deliberately no "whole route" button and no breadcrumb: closing the leg IS the whole route. RENDERED ONCE, EMPTY, AND HIDDEN. static/js/route_rail.js fills it for the route map.js's `activateRoute` opens and hides it again when the route detail sheet closes. Nothing here depends on which route that is, so the partial carries no route context at all — only the URL templates the script addresses the open route's endpoints with. INSIDE #map, NOT AFTER IT. The page body is a flex column and #map is its growing child, so a sibling after it would take height from the map and resize the canvas on every open. Positioned over the map's foot instead, it moves the map's bottom chrome by one token: static/css/map.css raises `--map-bottom-row-offset` while `#map[data-route-rail-open]` is set, and every bottom-anchored control reads that offset already. Inside #map also keeps a press on the rail from counting as a click outside the route detail sheet (static/js/map_sheet.js excludes #map), which would close the sheet and, with it, the rail. THE ACTIONS ARE A MENU. Five actions in a restricted area collapse into the "…" menu (design-system rule 5): Terrain and bulletin, then the routes row's own four in its order — includes/_route_rail_menu_items.html. The first opens the route detail sheet, which a tap no longer opens: the rail took the sheet's top half (name, figures, profile), and the sheet keeps what the rail does not show — the terrain lines and the day's bulletin reading. A pending share has only that first item. ITS OWN CLOSE, BECAUSE ITS LIFETIME IS ITS OWN. The rail opens on a tap and stays open while the sheet opens and closes over it, so it cannot borrow the sheet's dismissal. The × beside the menu is the one control for it, the sheet header's × drawn the same way. It is not a second action competing for rule 5's threshold: it dismisses the surface rather than acting on the route, the same place every map sheet keeps its ×. A PENDING SHARE'S SAVE SITS HERE. `[data-route-rail-claim]` holds the "Save route" / "Sign in to save this route" control map.js builds, shown for a pending share only: a recipient lands on the rail, and a Save one menu press away is a Save most recipients would never find. TICK LABELS ARE HTML, NOT SVG TEXT. The lane is stretched to the rail's width with `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, and SVG text scales with its viewBox, so a label drawn inside it would be squashed or stretched with the strip. The labels sit in `[data-route-rail-ticks]` under it, placed by percentage.
