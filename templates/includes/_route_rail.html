{% load i18n components %} {% comment %} includes/_route_rail.html — the route panel: the open route's profile, cut at its transitions, under a header for the route or a point (SNOW-1018, restacked by SNOW-1065, one panel since SNOW-1068, points only since 2026-10-02). A RAIL is the card pinned top-left while a route is open (docs/glossary.md). There is one: SNOW-1065 retired rail two, and SNOW-1068 folded the point card into it, because a point placed on the profile is read in the header right above it. The profile is always showing; the HEADER takes one of two states: route — the route's NAME as the title, its META LINE as the subtitle, the routes list's own format and strings ("12.9km · 337m ↑ · 1906m ↓ · 2h51m", routes/partials/_route.html), the time only when the recording has one; point — the aspect wheel and the reading (_route_point_card.html) in the title and subtitle's place, and the eyebrow becomes the route's name. Pressing the wheel clears the point. Then the LANE, the elevation profile drawn as one filled shape per leg under one outline, with distance ticks along its foot. 400 px wide on desktop, the map's width less its insets on a phone (`--route-column-width`, static/css/map.css), where a 13 km profile's shape reads rather than drawing near flat across a whole desktop. A TAP ON THE PROFILE'S TOP LINE places the point at that distance, with a generous margin either side of the line but not down in the fill; a tap on the route's line on the map places it too. The point is drawn on the lane as a vertical line with a dot where it crosses the outline, the point's ELEVATION at the top of the line and its DISTANCE from the start at the bottom (`[data-route-rail-readout]`), right of the line unless they would not fit. The legs are drawn and never pressed: leg selection, and dragging along the profile, were removed on 2026-10-02. RENDERED ONCE, EMPTY, AND HIDDEN. static/js/route_rail.js fills it for the route map.js's `activateRoute` opens and hides it again when the route detail sheet closes. Nothing here depends on which route that is, so the partial carries no route context at all — only the URL templates the script addresses the open route's endpoints with. INSIDE #map, NOT AFTER IT. The page body is a flex column and #map is its growing child, so a sibling after it would take height from the map and resize the canvas on every open. Pinned top-left instead — under the region chip's row on desktop, on the top edge on a phone, where that row is withdrawn while a route is open — it moves nothing else on the map: opening a route or placing a point changes only what the panel says (static/css/map.css). Inside #map also keeps a press on the rail from counting as a click outside the route detail sheet (static/js/map_sheet.js excludes #map), which would close the sheet and, with it, the rail. THE ACTIONS ARE A MENU. Five actions in a restricted area collapse into the "…" menu (design-system rule 5): Terrain, then the routes row's own four in its order — includes/_route_rail_menu_items.html. The first opens the route detail sheet, which a tap no longer opens: the rail took the sheet's top half (name, figures, profile), and the sheet keeps what the rail does not show — the terrain lines. A pending share has only that first item. ITS OWN CLOSE, BECAUSE ITS LIFETIME IS ITS OWN. The rail opens on a tap and stays open while the sheet opens and closes over it, so it cannot borrow the sheet's dismissal. The × beside the menu is the one control for it, the sheet header's × drawn the same way, and it always closes the route: the point header's wheel is what clears a point. It is not a second action competing for rule 5's threshold: it dismisses the surface rather than acting on the route, the same place every map sheet keeps its ×. A PENDING SHARE'S SAVE SITS HERE. `[data-route-rail-claim]` holds the "Save route" / "Sign in to save this route" control map.js builds, shown for a pending share only: a recipient lands on the rail, and a Save one menu press away is a Save most recipients would never find. TICK LABELS AND THE POINT'S READOUT ARE HTML, NOT SVG. The lane is stretched to the rail's width with `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, and SVG text and circles scale with its viewBox, so a label or a dot drawn inside it would be squashed or stretched with the strip. The tick labels sit in `[data-route-rail-ticks]` under it and the readout over it, placed by percentage.
