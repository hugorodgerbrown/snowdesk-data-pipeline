{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_ellipsis.html — the overflow glyph: three dots on a horizontal axis (SNOW-830). One source for one mark, the same reason _icon_pencil.html and _icon_share.html beside it are partials. Its caller is includes/_overflow_menu.html's trigger, which drew the literal "…" CHARACTER until this ticket. WHY A DRAWN GLYPH AND NOT THE CHARACTER. Two reasons, and the second is the one that forced it. A row's other controls (Share, the pencil, the trash) are all 17px SVGs on the 24-grid, and a text ellipsis next to them renders at the inherited font's own baseline, weight and dot spacing — visibly a different family of mark. And the character carried a workaround: written as its decimal numeric entity, every digit of that code point is also a valid hex digit, which ds-lint's hex-colour rule reads as a raw colour literal, so the partial had to explain why it used the bare character instead. A drawn glyph removes the problem rather than documenting it. HORIZONTAL, not vertical. The trigger sits at the end of a row whose other controls are side by side, and a vertical kebab reads as "this column continues downwards" — which on a scrolling list of rows is exactly the wrong suggestion. STYLE — the house stroke (SNOW-1434). Each dot is an r=1 circle under the 2px round-capped stroke, so it renders as a solid r=2 dot on this grid without a fill: the same mark the first, filled drawing made, inside the rule in docs/decisions/ui-icons-are-house-stroke-partials.md rather than an exception to it. A true outline, a hollow ring, was tried and rejected: at 17px a 2px ring closes to a smudge beside Share and the pencil. Centres 6 grid units apart, edges 2 apart, so the three keep visible air between them. Drawn for this ticket, so there is no third-party attribution to carry.
