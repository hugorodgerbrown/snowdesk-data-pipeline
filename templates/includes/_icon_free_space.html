{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_free_space.html — the "give this device its space back" glyph: an arrow off a line (SNOW-749). The exact inverse of includes/_icon_download_arrow.html, and paired with it on purpose. SNOW-749 splits removing a download into two verbs, and the pair has to read as opposites rather than as two shades of delete: down onto the line fetch this area onto this device up off the line take it off this device again — the AREA stays on the account, so it is one tap to bring back That is why this is not a second bin. The trash (includes/_icon_trash.html) means gone everywhere, and two bins on one row would make the difference between them invisible at a glance — which is exactly the difference the user has to judge before pressing one.
