{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_observation.html — the field-observation glyph: a pair of binoculars (SNOW-333 drew a warning triangle; extracted here by SNOW-658 and redrawn on Hugo's request). One source for one mark. The glyph identifies the field-observation roundel (#report-btn, public/partials/_map_embed.html) AND, at 22px, the header of the panel that roundel opens — Hugo's design makes the roundel's own icon the panel's only identity mark, so a reader can confirm which roundel they tapped. Why binoculars rather than the warning triangle it replaces: the triangle said "hazard", which is the bulletin's job — the danger scale, the choropleth and the problem cards all carry it, and it collided with the avalanche-problem iconography elsewhere on the page. NOTE ON STYLE — this glyph is FILLED (fill="currentColor", no stroke) on a 512 grid, whereas its two siblings (_icon_favourite.html's outline star, _icon_downloads.html's viewfinder) are 24-grid 2px strokes. It therefore reads heavier than they do at the same box size. That is a deliberate choice by Hugo, not an oversight — do not "fix" it by redrawing this as a stroke, which is the version it replaced.
