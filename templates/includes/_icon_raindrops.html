{% comment %} templates/includes/_icon_raindrops.html — the precipitation mark. Labels the hourly chart's precipitation baseline in the right gutter, the counterpart to _icon_snowflake.html one band above it (SNOW-723). Two drops rather than one, so the pair reads as rainfall rather than as a single droplet meaning "water". The two bands measure different things — centimetres of settled snow above, millimetres of water equivalent below — so they take different marks and different colours rather than one mark in two tints. Decorative, aria-hidden, colour inherited via currentColor; see the snowflake for the reasoning. Context: size : int — width and height in px (default 22). The chart draws it at 22 beside a baseline and at 15 inline in a summary line, where 22 would outweigh the figure it annotates. {% endcomment %} {% firstof size 22 as px %}
