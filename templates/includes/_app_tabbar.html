{% comment %} templates/includes/_app_tabbar.html — the installed phone app's bottom tab bar (SNOW-1476). Four tabs: Map, Today, Saved, You. Rendered on every page that extends public/base.html, but drawn ONLY in the installed app on a phone: the bar is `display: none` until the `(display-mode: standalone) and (max-width: 639.98px)` block in src/css/main.css shows it, and that same block hides the footer and the avatar menu the bar replaces. A phone browser, a tablet and a desktop never see it (docs/decisions/the-installed-phone-app-has-a-tab-bar.md). ON THE MAP the tabs are buttons: static/js/app_tabbar.js opens each tab's panel over the map (Today, the Saved sheets, the You sheet) and lights the tab whose panel is open. OFF THE MAP they are links to /map/?panel=…, which opens the map as it was left with that panel open. `{% app_tabbar_state %}` (apps/public/templatetags/app_tabbar.py) decides the tab lit on load: You on account, help and legal pages, Map elsewhere.
