{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} templates/includes/_weather_day_picker.html — region 2 of the location forecast page (SNOW-789). THE WEEK AS NAVIGATION. Seven cells carrying a weekday, a condition icon and a high/low, and nothing else: the cells answer "which day am I planning for", and everything below the picker describes the day the reader picked. Freezing level, wind and the snowfall chip were in the old strip's cells and are gone from here — they are per-day facts, so they belong to the selected day's own line and its meteogram, not to seven columns at once. **Every cell is a radio, which is the change at the heart of SNOW-789.** The strip this replaces gave a radio only to days carrying an hourly series (two of seven), so five columns were inert markup and the week was mostly undisplayable. ``day.selectable`` survives, but it decides only whether that day has a meteogram — the page reads it, this partial does not. CSS-only: there is no JavaScript, and the reveal below the picker is hand-written ``:has()`` in src/css/main.css. See the "Forecast day selector" block there for why it cannot be Tailwind's ``peer-checked:``. EACH INPUT/LABEL PAIR GETS ITS OWN WRAPPER, AND THAT IS LOAD-BEARING. ``peer-checked:`` compiles to the GENERAL sibling combinator, so with every input and label flat in one grid the checked day's input matches every LATER cell's label too and highlights the rest of the week. The wrapper bounds the ``~`` to one pair. Same trap as the reveal rules. SELECTION HAS TWO CHANNELS, AND NEITHER MOVES THE CELL. The border turns accent, and a marker bar under the low temperature goes from transparent to filled. The handoff asked for the border alone and argued colour was not the only channel because ``aria-current`` moved with the selection — but it cannot: the attribute means THE CURRENT DATE, so it is static on day 0, and a reader who cannot separate the accent border from the ordinary one had nothing left. The bar is the fix, and it is a bar rather than a ring or a weight change because those two resize the cell's contents as the reader steps along the week, which reads as the layout twitching rather than as a selection moving. The bar is always in the DOM at a fixed size, so only its fill changes. The rule that fills it is hand-written in src/css/main.css beside the reveal rules: the bar is a DESCENDANT of the label, not a sibling of the input, so no ``peer-*`` variant can reach it.
