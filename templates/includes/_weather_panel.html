The single-day surface, shared by every place weather appears server-side: the bulletin masthead (region centroid), the resort page (one panel per curated location) and the favourite detail card (the pin's own location). SNOW-761 rebuilt this as a **compact, fully tokenised row rather than the bucket-coloured hero** the pre-SNOW-762 partial carried. The hero was a full-bleed ``--color-weather-*`` band with a ``bg-black/50`` overlay and white text on top of it, and it owned the masthead's wayfinding as well — the ``

``, the eyebrow and the share button all lived inside the weather panel. That coupling is what made the strip remove the masthead too. This partial now renders weather and nothing else, carries no colour of its own, and can be dropped into any card; ``bulletin_header.html`` keeps its own heading and share button. The ``--color-weather-*`` tokens stay registered in the component library but are rendered by nothing — see the note in ``src/css/main.css``. Renders nothing at all when ``weather_display`` is falsy. That is the ordinary case for a historical date: ``Weather`` rows begin at the day the estate was first fetched, so a page for last February has no row and must degrade to no panel rather than to an error or an empty shell.
