{% extends "public/base.html" %} {% comment %} trips/trip.html — one trip's own page (SNOW-820). The organiser's view of the object: the day, the meeting time, the meeting point, the route drawn with a marker where the group meets, the distance and vertical figures, the elevation profile, and their own note. SNOW-821 adds the public share page, which renders the same summary and the same map from the same context builder — the difference between the two surfaces is who may see the trip, never what it says about it. SCOPED BY PARTICIPATION since SNOW-822 (apps/trips/views.py's trip_detail): everybody who has SAVED the trip gets this page, and what differs between the organiser and everyone else is which controls render. Somebody holding the LINK who has not saved it gets trips/trip_share.html instead — a different URL and a different surface, because the uuid in this one's address is the savers' identifier. SNOW-848 TOOK THE SOCIAL SURFACE OFF THIS PAGE. There was a roster card — "four people are going", the names, a Join/Leave control — and it is gone, along with the separate "Save route" control beside the map. A trip is a shareable item rather than a social event: it is planned, the link is sent, and whoever opens it saves it. Nobody sees who else holds it or how many do. The MODEL is untouched — ``TripParticipant`` still records every save, which is how ``Trip.objects.for_user`` knows whose agenda this belongs on — so do not read the rows being there as licence to print a count again. rather than on a page of its own: editing a trip is a correction to something already on screen — a time moved by half an hour, a note added — and sending the organiser to a second page to change one field would lose the context that told them it needed changing. No share card. Until SNOW-821 this page is reachable only by the account that created it, so a preview of it is meaningless to anyone who could see one — and it is behind auth, so no unfurler will ever reach it.
