{% extends "public/base.html" %} {% comment %} trips/trip_list.html — the reader's own trips (SNOW-823). A LIST PAGE, which is an exception to docs/decisions/two-documents-and-a-map.md and is argued there rather than here. In short: a route, a favourite and an observation are indexed SPATIALLY and the map is that index, which is why SNOW-803 could delete their list pages. A trip is indexed TEMPORALLY, and the map has no index for that — its scrubber picks a date to read the bulletin for, not a date to filter the reader's own objects by. A collection of routes is an inventory; a collection of trips is an agenda. Two sections, split against the trip's own date. Upcoming reads soonest-first because an agenda reads forwards; past reads most-recent-first because a history reads backwards. A trip dated TODAY is upcoming — the day it exists for has not finished. No share card: this page is one signed-in reader's own agenda, so a preview of it is meaningless to anyone who could see one, and it is behind auth so no unfurler will ever reach it. SNOW-848 REDREW EVERY ROW AS A CARD and took the social labelling off it. An upcoming trip now carries the whole answer — when, what, the organiser's note, the three figures and the meeting point — where the row it replaced gave a day, a name and a distance. The "You organised this" chip and the "N people going" line are gone: nobody sees who else holds a trip. A past trip stays a ROW, on the subtle surface, because it is a history rather than a plan. See _trip_card.html and _trip_past_row.html. Unpaginated by design. A person organises a handful of trips a season and the cap is 25 organised each; a page that long needs no pagination and no new index. WHERE CREATING A TRIP LANDS (SNOW-834). trip_create redirects here with ?created=, so this page both confirms the write and carries the Share control for the trip it just made.
