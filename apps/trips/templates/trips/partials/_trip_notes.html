FIRST CARD ON THE PAGE, above the details, and that is deliberate: the notes are the most human part of a shared trip. The day and the distance are facts anyone could read off the route; "bring a harness — there is a short exposed step below the shoulder" is the reason the organiser sent the link to these particular people. OMITTED ENTIRELY when there is no note, never rendered as an empty block. A trip with nothing written on it is a trip with nothing written on it, and a labelled blank claims otherwise. The note is rendered through ordinary Django auto-escaping. Nothing here calls ``mark_safe``, and nothing should: it is user-supplied prose (invariant 1). ``linebreaksbr`` turns the newlines the textarea captured into markup, which is a transform of whitespace only and does not admit any tag the escape pass has already neutralised.
