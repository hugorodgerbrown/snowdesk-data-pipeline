{% comment %} trips/partials/_trip_meeting_manual.html — the meeting point's two fields, now hidden, as the picker's transport. THE VISIBLE FIELDS ARE GONE (SNOW-840). This partial used to render two labelled number inputs inside a collapsed "Enter coordinates manually" panel, and its comment argued for them on three grounds: - a keyboard-only visitor, who cannot drag a MapLibre marker; - someone who already HAS a coordinate — read off a guidebook, a screenshot or another app — and wants to paste it rather than hunt for the same spot by eye; - a visitor with no JavaScript, for whom the two fields and their prefilled route-start values were the whole control. Those arguments were sound and they lost anyway. Hugo's judgement, seeing the picker running: "no one is going to use that". A control that almost nobody uses is not free — every organiser authoring a trip has to read the panel's title, work out whether it concerns them and dismiss it, which is a cost paid by everyone to serve very few. WHAT IT COSTS, recorded so nobody rediscovers it as a bug: a keyboard-only visitor can no longer choose a meeting point, and neither can a visitor with no JavaScript. Both get the route's own first coordinate — the value these fields still arrive prefilled with — and no way to move it. That is a real regression in reach, accepted deliberately. The answer when it is addressed is a keyboard affordance ON THE MAP, not the return of two number boxes underneath it; the second case (pasting a coordinate) is served by the map itself, since a coordinate someone holds can be found on it. THE INPUTS THEMSELVES REMAIN, because they are the form's actual transport. ``TripForm.latitude`` and ``.longitude`` are required, and ``static/js/trip_meeting_picker.js`` finds them by their ``data-meeting-*`` attributes and writes the marker's position into them on every move — the map is a control over these fields, never a replacement for them. Deleting them would post a trip with no meeting point. Rendered bare rather than through ``includes/_form_field.html``: a hidden input has no label, no help text and no error slot to hang anything on, and a field-level error on either is unreachable through the UI now that only the marker writes them.
