{% load i18n %} {% comment %} trips/partials/_trip_stats.html — Distance / Ascent / Descent, as a trio of labelled figures (SNOW-848). ONE PARTIAL, TWO SURFACES, and that is the reason it exists: the design draws the same three cells in the list card's body and again in the trip view's details footer, at the same label and value treatment. Two copies would drift on the first change to either. THE TRIO IS NOT ALWAYS THREE. ``ascent_m`` and ``descent_m`` are nullable — a GPX track with no elevation carries neither — and a cell with nothing in it is omitted rather than dashed. Asymmetry is honest: a missing figure is a fact about the track, not a hole in the layout. The label is includes/_meta_cell.html, the site's existing tiny-uppercase metadata label. The design specifies 10px for it; the site has no 10px token and deliberately has not since SNOW-253 bumped --text-meta from 9px to 11px so the WCAG AA 4.5:1 requirement applies at the text-3 colour. --text-caption (11px), which _meta_cell.html already carries, is the nearest honest value and the correct ROLE. THE UNIT IS CLOSED UP TO ITS VALUE AND THE THOUSANDS ARE NOT GROUPED — "1340m", not the "1,340 m" the design draws. That is this project's spelling of these three numbers wherever they appear (SNOW-830): ``route-distance`` / ``route-ascent`` / ``route-descent`` in static/js/map_state.js spell them for the routes panel and the map popup, and the figures line in _trip_map.html spells them for the route card on this very page. A reader meeting the same measurement on four surfaces should not meet four spellings, and a card that said "2,192 m" here and "2192m ↓" two cards down would read as two measurements. Context: trip : Trip — the trip whose route figures these are. {% endcomment %}
