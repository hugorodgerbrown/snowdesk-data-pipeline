{% load i18n components snowdesk_time %} {% comment %} public/partials/_place_sheet_body.html — what a tapped resort pin or saved pin's phone sheet says (SNOW-1100). Rendered by ``apps.public.place_sheet`` (``resort_sheet_partial`` and ``favourite_sheet_partial``) and swapped by ``static/js/map_place_sheet.js`` into ``#place-sheet``, an ``includes/_overlay_sheet.html`` that declares the detents peek, half and full. One fragment serves both kinds and all three detents, ordered so that each detent shows the next part of it: 1. PEEK (``data-sheet-peek``) — ONE ROW, the region sheet's: the region's danger tile, the place's name over the region's code badge, the level and the region's name, then the actions and the ×. A resort's action is the Save star; a saved pin's are the panel row's own "…" menu (``_favourite_row_menu_items.html``: Share → Rename → Delete). The header is ``data-sheet-drag`` and, for a saved pin, also the row the inline rename edits (``data-row-renameable``). 2. HALF (ends under ``data-sheet-half``) — the day's weather at the place. The level is already on the peek row. 3. FULL — the region's avalanche problems, the week at the place, a resort's curated details, and the links onward. With none of those there is nothing for FULL to show, so ``data-sheet-detents`` drops it. Context: kind — "resort" or "favourite". name — the place's name; "" for an unnamed pin. region — MicroRegion or None. day_rating — RegionDayRating for target_date, or None. level_label — the danger level's name, or "". target_date — the day the map is showing. bulletin_url — that day's bulletin page, or "". problem_cards — the bulletin's problem cards, possibly annotated. location — the place's Location, or None. weather_display — build_weather_display's result, or None. forecast_panel — build_point_forecast_panel's result, or None. forecast_url — the location's forecast page, or "". resort — the Resort (a resort, or a saved resort), or None. resort_url — the resort page, or "". favourite — the Favourite (kind "favourite" only), with ``w3w_share_text`` hung on it. can_favourite, favourited, favourite_uuid, signin_url, is_staff — the resort's Save star (kind "resort" only). {% endcomment %} {% trans "Unnamed pin" as unnamed_pin %} {% firstof name unnamed_pin as label %}
