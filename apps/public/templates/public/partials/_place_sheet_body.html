{% load i18n components resort_tags snowdesk_time %} {% comment %} public/partials/_place_sheet_body.html — what a tapped resort pin or saved pin's phone sheet says (SNOW-1100). Rendered by ``apps.public.place_sheet`` (``resort_sheet_partial`` and ``favourite_sheet_partial``) and swapped by ``static/js/map_place_sheet.js`` into ``#place-sheet``, an ``includes/_overlay_sheet.html`` that declares the detents peek, half and full. One fragment serves both kinds and all three detents, ordered so that each detent shows the next part of it: 1. PEEK (``data-sheet-peek``) — the header ROW: the round pin tile (``includes/_resort_tile.html``, a place's shape in search; the square danger tile is a region's), the place's name, the actions and the ×. The day is not in the peek: a resort's dated eyebrows name it ("Avalanche bulletin • SLF … Today", includes/_dated_eyebrow.html), a saved pin's weather line and bulletin link already did, and the full-width "Data from " row that sat here went (Hugo, 29 Oct 2026: "Date is everywhere on the page"). Nothing else — no level, code or region in the header (Hugo, 21 Oct: it could not be told from the region sheet, and had too much squeezed in). A resort's action is the Save star; a saved pin's are the panel row's own "…" menu (``_favourite_row_menu_items.html``: Share → Rename → Delete). The header is ``data-sheet-drag`` and, for a saved pin, also the row the inline rename edits (``data-row-renameable``). 2. HALF (ends under ``data-sheet-half``) — for a RESORT, where the sheet OPENS (Hugo, 27 Oct 2026; ``map_place_sheet.js`` sets ``data-detent-initial``): the bulletin callout ("Avalanche bulletin • SLF", the region's level or "No bulletin available on this date"), then the weather section down to today's tile for the chosen point. The curated line moved into the folded "About this resort". For a saved pin, which still opens at PEEK: the region's level, then the day's weather at the place and "Full forecast →". 3. FULL — a resort's week, Base / Mid / Summit chips, "View hourly forecast →" and "About this resort"; a saved pin's avalanche problems, then the links onward: the day's bulletin (primary) and the resort page (secondary). With none of those there is nothing for FULL to show, so ``data-sheet-detents`` drops it. A tap on the handle at FULL steps down to PEEK. WHAT THE SHEET DOES NOT SAY (SNOW-1161, docs/decisions/the-map-opens-a- resort-in-the-sheet.md). The sheet is "this place, on the map's day"; the resort page is "this resort, today". So the full facts list, the week, the season and every blank state belong to the page: no "About this resort" list (the summary line reads the same formatter as the page's card), no "The week" (the sheet already follows the scrubber, and a week inside it read as a second date control), no sign-up prompt or curation hint where "why it matters" is unwritten, and no dashed placeholder anywhere. Context: kind — "resort" or "favourite". name — the place's name; "" for an unnamed pin. region — MicroRegion or None. day_rating — RegionDayRating for target_date, or None. level_label — the danger level's name, or "". target_date — the day the map is showing. bulletin_url — that day's bulletin page, or "". problem_cards — the bulletin's problem cards, possibly annotated. location — the place's Location, or None. forecast_source — the NearestForecast the weather is borrowed from: the curated point nearest the pin (Hugo, 2026-10-28 — a pin buys no fetch of its own), or None. weather_display — build_weather_display's result for that point's row for the day, or None (always None on a forward day). forward_day — on a forward day, today's forecast entry for it, or None. forecast_url — that point's forecast page, or "". resort — the Resort (a resort, or a saved resort), or None. resort_url — the resort page, or "". favourite — the Favourite (kind "favourite" only), with ``w3w_share_text`` hung on it. signin_url — the resort's sign-in star (kind "resort" only).
