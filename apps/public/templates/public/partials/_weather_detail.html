Server-rendered by ``apps.public.api.weather_detail`` and injected into the map's weather sheet by ``static/js/map_weather_detail.js`` when a condition symbol on the map is tapped. THE SHEET IS THE PAGE (Hugo, 24 Oct 2026). Under its own peek row the sheet renders the location forecast page's body, ``public/partials/_weather_body.html``: the week as a day picker opened on the map's day, the selected day's line, its meteogram and the provenance line. It replaced a card holding one day and a "This week →" link to the page (SNOW-761, SNOW-1101), the same move SNOW-1164 made for the region and resort sheets (docs/decisions/the-sheet-is-the-page.md). The page's masthead, with its onward links, stays in the page's frame. Split across the phone detents (SNOW-1101; docs/decisions/sheet-detents-are-phone-only.md): PEEK — the symbol, " · " and the ×; the "Weather • Open-Meteo HH:MM" eyebrow, which names the map's day at its right (includes/_dated_eyebrow.html; the full-width "Data from" row it replaced went on 29 Oct 2026); then the high/low, new snow and wind of that day (Hugo, 21 Oct). The block is ``data-sheet-peek`` and ``data-sheet-drag``. HALF — down to the end of the week (``data-sheet-half`` on the picker). FULL — the selected day: its line, its meteogram when it has hours, and the provenance. A one-day (backfilled) row has no picker; it rises to FULL only when it has a meteogram. A day with no row is PEEK. The "Where this is" rows sit under all three, so a panel that names a resort or region always rises to FULL — otherwise a day with no row would strand them below the peek, where nothing can scroll to them. A FORWARD DAY IS A FORECAST, AND SAYS SO (SNOW-1105). A day after today has no row of its own; the view reads today's row and opens its picker on the matching entry. The peek line opens with "Forecast", and a note under it states when the run was issued — what the week looked like this morning, not a record of the day. Nothing here is user-supplied: a Location's name is curated and a Weather row comes from Open-Meteo, so there is no untrusted text on this surface. Context: location — locations.Location the reading belongs to. heading — str: the location's name, or a coordinate fallback. day — ForecastPanelDay for the requested date, or None: the day's own row on today or before, today's ``forecast`` entry after it (apps.weather.services.weather_display.build_point_forecast_panel). is_forecast — bool: the requested date is after today. issued_at — datetime the row was last fetched, or None. target_date — datetime.date the sheet was opened for. another_day — bool: the target date is not today. place_resort — Resort the point stands for, or None. place_region — MicroRegion the point is in, or None. place_region_url — str: that region's bulletin page for the day, or "". weather, panel, show_day_picker, selected_index, hourly_charts, day_strips, day_figures — the page's own forecast context (apps.public.views._location_forecast_context), passed to the shared body. {% endcomment %}
