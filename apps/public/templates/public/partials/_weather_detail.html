Server-rendered by ``apps.public.api.weather_detail`` and injected into the map's weather sheet by ``static/js/map_weather_detail.js`` when a condition symbol on the map is tapped. THIS IS A CARD, NOT A PAGE. It briefly carried the outlook chart, the seven-day strip and per-day hourly tables; that was too much for a surface reached by tapping a symbol, and it was the wrong place for all three. What is left answers one question — what is it doing there, now — and hands the rest to the full forecast behind a link. (None of those three components still exists: SNOW-789 rebuilt that page around a day picker.) The trim is also what the payload wanted: the hourly tables alone were 79% of this response. Content, in the order a skier reads it: 1. Who and when — location name, elevation, the day being shown. 2. Today's conditions — the symbol, the high/low, and the figures that change a plan (freezing level, snowfall, wind, precipitation chance). 3. A link to the full forecast, when the location has one. Nothing here is user-supplied: a Location's name is curated and a Weather row comes from Open-Meteo, so there is no untrusted text on this surface. Context: location — locations.Location the reading belongs to. heading — str: the location's name, or a coordinate fallback. weather — weather.Weather row for the requested date, or None. display — WeatherDisplay for that row, or None (apps.weather.services.weather_display.build_weather_display). target_date — datetime.date the sheet was opened for. forecast_url — str: URL of the full forecast for this location, or "". Empty for a location with no page of its own — see the view. The link is omitted rather than pointed somewhere that is not a forecast. {% endcomment %}
