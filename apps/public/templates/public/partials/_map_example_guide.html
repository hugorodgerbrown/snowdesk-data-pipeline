The welcome card's "Explore an example route" goes to routes:example, which puts the example share in the session and lands here with ?route_share=&example=1&d=. The route opens as any followed share does; this partial is the guide over it, and static/js/map_example_guide.js runs it. Four steps — say what just happened to the date, follow the track, zoom in until the line shows the slope, read the colours — then a finish card, and the map back on today. Nothing is locked (Hugo, SNOW-1230): close the route or wander off and the bar folds into the paused pill, which keeps its step; change the date and the pill offers the way back to the example's day. Exit, Finish and Done always put the map back on today and say so. Everything a reader sees is rendered here so {% trans %} reaches it. The JS fills in only the day ([data-example-day], formatted in the reader's locale), the route's name ([data-example-name]), the step count and the zoom bar.
