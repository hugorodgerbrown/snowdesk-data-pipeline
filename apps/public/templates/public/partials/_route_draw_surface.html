{% load i18n static components %} {% comment %} public/partials/_route_draw_surface.html — draw a route on the map (SNOW-1256, behind the ``route_drawing`` flag). The routes panel's "Create a route" (public/partials/_route_add_rows.html) closes that panel and opens this one. While it is open the map is a drawing surface: every app overlay steps aside (window.PlacementFocus, the same focus the pin pickers use), swisstopo's ski-touring routes are drawn under the line as a guide, and a press adds a waypoint. HOW A POINT IS PLACED differs by pointer, on purpose (decided 27 Oct 2026, phone first): * on a phone (``pointer: coarse``) the map moves under a still cross and "Add point" drops a waypoint where the cross is — the same "move the map under a still pin" model a favourite and a meeting point are placed by (docs/decisions/a-place-is-chosen-by-moving-the-map.md), because a fingertip covers the very ground it is trying to choose; * with a mouse a click on the map adds the point under the cursor, and the cross and the Add button are not shown. FOLLOWING THE SKI ROUTES (SNOW-1277). From z12 the panel draws swisstopo's ski-touring network (/api/ski-network.geojson) as a faint shadow and a tap near a line snaps to it, the leg routed along the network when the last point is on it too; the "Follow ski routes" switch turns that off, and then the waypoints are joined by straight lines. After each change the waypoints are posted to routes:draw_preview, which samples the terrain under the line and answers with the Feature the routes layer itself draws — so the legs, the slope classes and the profile here are the saved route's, not an approximation of them. A FORM PANEL, in the design system's three kinds: a task, opened from the routes panel, so no detents and no drag-to-close. Escape, the × and another panel opening all close it, and closing keeps the draft on this device. Drawing needs no account; saving does. An anonymous drawer's Save button is a sign-in link, and the draft waits in localStorage for them to come back. SAVING OFFLINE (SNOW-1262). Save works without a signal: the route is put in the mutation queue under a key the draft is tagged with, the routes panel lists it as "Waiting to sync" and it replays on reconnect, with or without a tab open. Reopened while it waits, this panel shows the line with every edit and Save disabled and the "save-queued" line below, so the same route cannot be queued twice. See static/js/route_draw.js's header. Included from map.html only while the flag is active for the request.
