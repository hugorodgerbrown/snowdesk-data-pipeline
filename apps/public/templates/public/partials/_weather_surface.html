Mirrors _report_surface.html and _favourites_surface.html: an empty sheet container plus the one script that fills it. There is no roundel and no trigger button here. The trigger is the weather symbol on the map itself — map.js binds the tap on the ``weather-point`` layer and calls ``window.pwaWeatherDetail.open()``. That is why this sheet, unlike the three UGC ones, has nothing to render until something is tapped. DETENTS (SNOW-1101). On a phone the sheet rests at PEEK — the day's line: the symbol, the name, the high/low, new snow and wind — and rises to HALF for the rest of the card. There is no FULL: the card is small, and the week and the hourly detail live on the forecast page it links to. A day with no row narrows the detents to peek (``data-sheet-detents`` on the body). It does not take ``scroll_body``, as the region and place sheets do not: the card fits, and the sheet scrolls as one block if it ever must.
