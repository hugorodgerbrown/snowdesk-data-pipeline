Mirrors _report_surface.html and _favourites_surface.html: an empty sheet container plus the one script that fills it. There is no roundel and no trigger button here. The trigger is the weather symbol on the map itself — map.js binds the tap on the ``weather-point`` layer and calls ``window.pwaWeatherDetail.open()``. That is why this sheet, unlike the three UGC ones, has nothing to render until something is tapped. ``scroll_body=True``, as the other sheets use: the header stays pinned and only the body between it and the sheet's bottom edge scrolls. The body this sheet injects (public/partials/_weather_detail.html) therefore carries ``flex-1 overflow-y-auto min-h-0`` on its own scroll region — a map overlay that grows with its content pushes its own footer off screen, which is the defect the bounded-height rule exists to stop.
