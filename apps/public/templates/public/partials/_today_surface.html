Today's bulletin danger for the regions the reader has pinned, and the weather at the resorts they have pinned, each resort nested under its region (favourites/partials/_today.html, built by apps.favourites.today.build_today). Always today, whatever day the map is on: the panel answers "what is it like at my places this morning", and the date row beside its trigger is where the map's own day is chosen. Opened by the sun roundel in the date row (#map-today-panel-toggle in _map_embed.html), which it sits beside, so on a desktop it docks in the left column under search (``side="left"``), as the region, place and weather sheets do; on a phone it docks full width. A roundel panel: no detents, and the roundel toggles it (docs/design-system.md, "What opened a map panel decides its kind"). A row opens that region's or resort's own sheet in its place — one panel at a time — and map_panel_trail.js gives that sheet a back pill that reopens Today. Rendered only for a signed-in reader, the one who can have pinned anything (``favourites_eligible``); static/js/today_panel.js drives it.
