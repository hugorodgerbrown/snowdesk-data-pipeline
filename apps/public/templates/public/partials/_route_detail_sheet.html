Two sheets, two surfaces: #route-sheet is the list of the user's routes, this one is one route's detail. WHY IT IS NOT A POPUP ANY MORE. A route's detail was a 320px MapLibre popup, and five tickets of terrain reading went into it — distance, ascent, descent, the steepest angle and steep length (SNOW-961), the no-fall passages (SNOW-964), the crux count (SNOW-911), the fall-line arrows (SNOW-974) and an inline elevation profile. On a real route the figures wrapped to four lines above the chart and the card covered a good part of the map it was describing. Widening the popup was not available: ``maxWidth`` is set on map.js's shared ``mountDetailPopup`` and ``.detail-popup`` is chrome the resort pin and the favourite card also use, so a route's figures are not a reason to reshape those. They keep the popup; a route gets the sheet the other five surfaces already use, with the map flown to the track above it. The sheet is also the first surface with room for the route's BULLETIN READING — where the line crosses the ground each of the day's avalanche problems names — which has been built and shown for trips since SNOW-839 and never for a route. Shaped on _weather_detail.html, which is the closest precedent: the only other sheet opened by a MAP TAP rather than a roundel, and the only other one that injects server-rendered HTML. Hence ``scroll_body=True`` with the header pinned and ``flex-1 overflow-y-auto min-h-0`` on the body — a map overlay that grows with its content pushes its own header off screen.
