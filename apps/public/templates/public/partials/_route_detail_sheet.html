WHY IT IS NOT A POPUP ANY MORE. A route's detail was a 320px MapLibre popup, and five tickets of terrain reading went into it — distance, ascent, descent, the steepest angle and steep length (SNOW-961), the no-fall passages (SNOW-964), a key-passage count (SNOW-911; removed with the rest of the route-level cruxes by SNOW-1066), the fall-line arrows (SNOW-974) and an inline elevation profile. On a real route the figures wrapped to four lines above the chart and the card covered a good part of the map it was describing. Widening the popup was not available: ``maxWidth`` is set on map.js's shared ``mountDetailPopup`` and ``.detail-popup`` is chrome the resort pin and the favourite card also use, so a route's figures are not a reason to reshape those. They keep the popup; a route gets the sheet the other five surfaces already use, with the map flown to the track above it. Shaped on _weather_detail.html, the only other sheet opened from the map rather than a roundel.
