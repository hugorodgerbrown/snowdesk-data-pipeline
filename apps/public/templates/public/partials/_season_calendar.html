{% load i18n snowdesk_time %} {% comment %} The season calendar (SNOW-1158) — the ONE drawing of a region's season, day by day, used everywhere a season calendar appears: the resort page's season section, the bulletin page's season sheet (served as the HTMX fragment from season_calendar_partial), the /help/ illustration and the component library. There is deliberately no second copy of this markup, so the pages cannot drift apart. Layout. One markup, re-laid out by CSS alone (src/css/main.css, "Season calendar"): in a wide container the months are rows (Nov … Apr), a month label column, then one dot per day against a 1 · 5 · 10 … 30 axis; in a narrow one (a container query, so the sheet and the resort column each decide by their own width) the months become columns of short bars with the axis down the left. Cell states (data-state on .season-day): "none" — no bulletin, outlined; a rating key — the EAWS fill; "split" — two levels in one day, data-lo left and data-hi (the peak) right; "future" — not yet, a faint tint. data-level is always the day's PEAK (RegionDayRating.max_rating — the compressed-views rule, docs/compressed-views-rating-rule.md). One day may carry .season-day-ring, the 2px ring — always the day the page shows (SNOW-1163): the resort page's today or dated day, server-side; the bulletin page's own day, added by static/js/season_sheet.js from #season-grid's data-selected-date so the cached fragment stays one body. Two variants, by ``linked``: * not linked (resort page, help, library) — the calendar is ONE role="img" whose aria-label is the season summary; cells carry a title ("14 Jan 2026 · Considerable") and nothing inside is a link. * linked (bulletin page) — each day with a bulletin is a link to that day (aria-label + title); the calendar is not an image, because an image's children are presentational and the links would vanish from the accessibility tree. One tap on a day follows its link straight to that day's bulletin (SNOW-1163). Both variants share one key (SNOW-1163): the colours alone — No bulletin, Low … High, Very high only when a day reached it — with no counts and no entries for the split cell or the ring. Context: season_months — SeasonMonths (apps.public.season_calendar.build_season_months). season_summary — SeasonSummary: the aria-label (not linked) and the key's levels. linked — truthy on the bulletin page. region — MicroRegion (canonical_region_id + name_slug), only read when ``linked``. {% endcomment %}
