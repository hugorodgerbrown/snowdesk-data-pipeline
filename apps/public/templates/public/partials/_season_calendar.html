{% load i18n snowdesk_time %} {% comment %} The season calendar (SNOW-1158) — the ONE drawing of a region's season, day by day, used everywhere a season calendar appears: the resort page's season section, the bulletin page's season sheet (served as the HTMX fragment from season_calendar_partial), the /help/ illustration and the component library. There is deliberately no second copy of this markup, so the pages cannot drift apart. Layout. One markup, re-laid out by CSS alone (src/css/main.css, "Season calendar"): in a wide container the months are rows (Nov … Apr), a month label column, then one dot per day against a 1 · 5 · 10 … 30 axis; in a narrow one (a container query, so the sheet and the resort column each decide by their own width) the months become columns of short bars with the axis down the left. Cell states (data-state on .season-day): "none" — no bulletin, outlined; a rating key — the EAWS fill; "split" — two levels in one day, data-lo left and data-hi (the peak) right; "future" — not yet, a faint tint. data-level is always the day's PEAK (RegionDayRating.max_rating — the compressed-views rule, docs/compressed-views-rating-rule.md); the bulletin sheet's readout reads it. One day may carry .season-day-ring, the 2px ring: the resort page's today / first High day, server-side; the bulletin page's selected day, added by static/js/season_sheet.js from #season-grid's data-selected-date so the cached fragment stays one body. Two variants, by ``linked``: * not linked (resort page, help, library) — the calendar is ONE role="img" whose aria-label is the season summary; cells carry a title ("14 Jan 2026 · Considerable") and nothing inside is a link. The key is the short one: No bulletin, Low … High. * linked (bulletin page) — each day with a bulletin is a link to that day (aria-label + title); the calendar is not an image, because an image's children are presentational and the links would vanish from the accessibility tree. The key is the full one: a count per level, "Two levels in one day" and "Selected day". Every cell also carries its date in the readout's two forms, data-day-long ("Monday 6 April 2026") and data-day-short ("6 April"), formatted here in the active language: with JS a click selects a day rather than following its link (static/js/season_sheet.js), and the readout reads them, so the cached fragment stays one body per day and no date string is built in JavaScript. Without JS the links navigate. Context: season_months — SeasonMonths (apps.public.season_calendar.build_season_months). season_summary — SeasonSummary: the aria-label (not linked) and the key's levels and counts. linked — truthy on the bulletin page. region — MicroRegion (canonical_region_id + name_slug), only read when ``linked``. {% endcomment %}
