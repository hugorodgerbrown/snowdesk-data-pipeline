The picker drops to six or five cells when its own width cannot hold seven (the same file, "Forecast day picker width"). IN THE MAP (``in_map``) the picker carries ``data-sheet-half``: on a phone the sheet's HALF detent is the peek plus the week, and FULL is the selected day under it. A one-day row marks no half: the sheet's HALF fits what it has, up to half the screen.
