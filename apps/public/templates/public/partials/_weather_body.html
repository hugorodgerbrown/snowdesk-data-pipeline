Everything under the masthead and the day's summary line: the forecast note on a forward day, the day picker, the selected day's line, its linked strips (SNOW-1219) or figures, the provenance line and the "Where this is" rows. The page wraps it in the site's frame under ``includes/_weather_masthead.html`` and ``includes/_weather_day_summary.html``; the sheet wraps it under its peek row, which holds those two and the map's day. A fact about a location's weather is added here, so both show it — since 25 Oct 2026 the page draws the sheet's strips too, where it used to draw the meteogram (Hugo: "it should be the same content"). Three shapes, discriminated by ``show_day_picker`` and NEVER by the date: live row the week; days 1–2 reveal a day line AND a meteogram, days 3–7 reveal a day line alone. backfilled row ``forecast=None`` is one day, so no picker: the day line and its meteogram render plainly, with no hiding class — with no radio the ``:has()`` reveal could never match. no row at all the "nothing recorded" message, or in the map on a day after today, "the forecast does not reach this day yet". The reveal is CSS-only — see the "Forecast day selector" block in src/css/main.css. The picker drops to six or five cells when its own width cannot hold seven (the same file, "Forecast day picker width"). The day lines are BRIEF whenever there are strips or figures under them: those state the freezing level, so the line keeps only the date, the condition and the daylight. IN THE MAP (``in_map``) the picker carries ``data-sheet-half``: on a phone the sheet's HALF detent is the peek plus the week, and FULL is the selected day under it. A one-day row marks no half: the sheet's HALF fits what it has, up to half the screen.
