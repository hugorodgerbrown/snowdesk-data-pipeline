SNOW-660: ``data-default-date`` (today) is gone. It seeded the ribbon's displayed date, which meant a visitor who had asked for nothing arrived at a map coloured for a day the server had picked. The displayed date now comes from ``?d=`` alone, and no date means no date — see map_season_ribbon.js. This element is the data carrier (season bounds + default region) and the caption header. The visible cells are painted by map.js's seasonRibbonInit IIFE into the SCRUBBER's own track once the season-ratings cache resolves, so they are decorative rather than interactive — the scrubber thumb they sit behind is what the user drags, and the ribbon just colours the ground it runs over. ``snowdesk:region-selected`` swaps the cells to the new region; ``snowdesk:date-changed`` keeps the active-day highlight in sync. There was a ``snowdesk:scrub-to`` click-to-scrub contract here until SNOW-615: no cell had carried a click handler since the cells moved into the track, so the documented event had no dispatcher and map.js's listener for it was dead. {% endcomment %} {% if ribbon %}
