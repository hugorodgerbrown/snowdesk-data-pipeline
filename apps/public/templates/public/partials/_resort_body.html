{% load i18n %} {% comment %} public/partials/_resort_body.html — a resort's body, on its page and in the map's place sheet (SNOW-1164). ONE BODY, TWO CONTAINERS. ``public/resort.html`` wraps it in the page's frame (the header with its eyebrow, title, tagline and actions); ``public/partials/_place_sheet_body.html`` wraps it in the sheet, under the one-row peek. On a phone the sheet at full height therefore IS the page, and the two cannot drift: a fact about a resort is added here, never to the sheet or the page alone. Context from ``apps.public.views.build_resort_body``. The sections, in one order whatever the season: the forecast — the resort's own data — then, in the map, the curated facts, then one row naming the avalanche region the resort sits in. On the page the facts are a sidebar on a desktop and the last section on a phone. A RESORT HAS NO AVALANCHE DANGER OF ITS OWN (Hugo, 25 Oct 2026). Until then this body carried the region's bulletin card, the day's problem cards and the season's danger calendar (SNOW-1156, SNOW-1164, SNOW-1126), so a resort's panel read as a second copy of its region's. They are the region's, and live on its panel and page; the resort keeps the one row (``includes/_place_link_row.html``) that opens it, with no level on it. ``in_map`` (set by the place sheet) keeps the body to one column whatever the viewport, since the desktop inset panel is a phone's width, and tightens the gap between sections to the sheet's. Context (beyond what each included partial documents): region — the resort's MicroRegion. bulletin_url — the region's bulletin page for the day shown. target_date — the day shown; ``is_today`` — whether it is today. forecast_points — the forecast's (``_resort_forecast.html``). in_map — bool: rendered inside the map's place sheet. {% endcomment %}
