The sections, in the order the season gives them (SNOW-1156): in season (a panel for the day) the bulletin card, the day's problems judged against the resort's height, then the forecast and the season calendar; out of season the forecast leads and the card follows. Then the curated facts — a sidebar on a desktop page, the last section everywhere else. ``in_map`` (set by the place sheet) keeps the body to one column whatever the viewport, since the desktop inset panel is a phone's width, and tightens the gap between sections to the sheet's.
