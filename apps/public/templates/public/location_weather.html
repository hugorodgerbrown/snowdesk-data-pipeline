THE PAGE THE MAP CARD HANDS OFF TO. Tapping a weather symbol opens a card carrying today's conditions and a "View forecast" link; this is what that link opens, and it holds everything the card deliberately drops — the week, and the hourly detail at full resolution. The card is a glance; this is the read. It is keyed on ``Location`` rather than on a resort or a region because most of the estate is neither. 461 of the 540 public locations are ``MicroRegion`` centroids: before this page they had no destination at all, so the card's link would have been missing on the large majority of taps. **THE PAGE IS THE MAP'S WEATHER SHEET** (Hugo, 25 Oct 2026: "it exists for deep link / SEO, but it should be the same content").
