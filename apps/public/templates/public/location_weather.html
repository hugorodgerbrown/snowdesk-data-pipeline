THE PAGE THE MAP CARD HANDS OFF TO. Tapping a weather symbol opens a card carrying today's conditions and a "View forecast" link; this is what that link opens, and it holds everything the card deliberately drops — the outlook chart, the seven-day strip, and the hourly detail at full resolution. The card is a glance; this is the read. It is keyed on ``Location`` rather than on a resort or a region because most of the estate is neither. 461 of the 540 public locations are ``MicroRegion`` centroids: before this page they had no destination at all, so the card's link would have been missing on the large majority of taps. Sections, top to bottom: 1. Heading — the location's own name if curated, else the resort or region it stands for. Never its coordinates (see the view). 2. Today — includes/_weather_panel.html. 3. The shape of the week — includes/_forecast_chart.html. 4. The figures behind it — includes/_forecast_panel.html, the day strip. 5. Today, hour by hour — includes/_hourly_chart.html (SNOW-786). This replaced the 24-row table the strip used to carry per day: both answered the same question, and the table only existed because there was no chart. 5. Onward links — the resort page and the region bulletin, where either exists. A centroid has only the second. Context: location — locations.Location. heading — str, resolved by the view. resort — regions.Resort the location belongs to, or None. region — regions.MicroRegion the location is the centroid of, or None. observed_on — datetime.date being shown. weather — weather.Weather row, or None. display — WeatherDisplay, or None. panel — ForecastPanel, or None. chart — ForecastChart, or None. hourly_chart — HourlyChart for observed_on, or None.
