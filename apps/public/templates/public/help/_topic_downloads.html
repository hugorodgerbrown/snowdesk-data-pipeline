Content partial for the help page; included via the body_template parameter of includes/_collapsible_panel.html. Documents the downloads roundel and its sheet (public/partials/_map_custom_download_control.html, _map_downloads_sheet.html, SNOW-522/588/634/635/645) plus the per-region download button in the ribbon header (_map_download_control.html), none of which had a help section. The distinction this panel exists to make is which of the two things a download is: it stores BASEMAP TILES for an area. Pages and avalanche data are cached separately, by visiting them — that half is the "Install & offline" panel's subject, and each panel points at the other rather than restating it. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A download saves the background map for an area onto this device, so the map still draws where there is no signal. The framed-square button on the right of the map opens "Downloads on this device": everything you have stored, what it is costing you, and the way to add more. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} There are two ways to store an area. Select a region on the map and use the download button beside its name in the header to take the whole region; or use "Download a custom area" inside the sheet, which lets you frame any part of the map yourself — the right choice for a tour that straddles a boundary. The sheet lists the two kinds separately, and custom areas can be renamed. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A budget at the top of the sheet caps how much space downloads may take, and you choose the figure. When a new download would take you past it, the oldest areas are removed to make room and you are asked first. Lowering the budget below what you already hold puts you over it, and the sheet says so rather than deleting anything behind your back. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Downloads live in this browser on this device. They do not follow your account to your phone, they are not part of your favourites or your routes, and clearing the site's data or resetting local data removes them. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The "Display on the map" switch at the foot of the sheet shades the areas you hold, so you can see the edges of your own coverage before you set off. It is the only thing that turns that shading off again. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} An area is downloaded for the base map that was showing at the time. Switching to a different base map is switching to a map you have not stored, and the sheet offers to download the same area again for it. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Downloading needs a connection; listing and removing what you already hold works offline, which is when space usually runs short. A download can also fail because the device is out of room or the area is larger than the budget allows — frame a smaller area and try again. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A download stores the map, not the avalanche bulletins. Bulletin pages and the danger colours are kept for offline use as you visit them — see "Install & offline" below. {% endblocktrans %}
