{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk can be installed as an app on your phone or computer. Look for an "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" option in your browser's address bar or share menu — once installed, it opens in its own window without any browser chrome. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Pages you've already visited load instantly on repeat visits, even with a poor connection, because the app shell is cached on your device. Avalanche data itself is always fetched fresh from the network — Snowdesk never shows you a stale danger rating from the cache. {% endblocktrans %}
