{% blocktrans trimmed %} Every bulletin page has a "Season" button in its header, marked with the season's years. It opens a heatmap of the whole season for that region: one tile per day from the season's start through to tomorrow, laid out as weeks running down the columns. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Each tile carries that day's danger rating in its colour, so a season's worth of conditions reads at a glance — the settled spells, and the weeks that ran high. A day whose rating varied across the region is split diagonally between the lowest and the highest it reached. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Tap any day that has a bulletin to jump straight to it. Days with no bulletin — before the season began, or a gap in what the provider published — are drawn flat and do nothing. {% endblocktrans %}
