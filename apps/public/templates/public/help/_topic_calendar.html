{% blocktrans trimmed %} Every bulletin page has a "Season" button in its header, marked with the season's years. It opens a calendar of the whole season for that region: one dot per day from the season's start, each month a row — or, on a phone, a column of short bars. The days are grouped in fives, so the 13th is the third day after the gap marked 11. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Each day carries its danger rating in its colour, so a season's worth of conditions reads at a glance — the settled spells, and the weeks that ran high. A day that held two levels is split in half, the lower on the left and the highest it reached on the right. Days still to come are drawn faint. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Tap any day that has a bulletin to jump straight to it; the day you are reading is ringed. Days with no bulletin — before the season began, or a gap in what the provider published — are drawn as an empty outline and do nothing. {% endblocktrans %}
