{% blocktrans trimmed %} Field observations are quick reports of what the ground is like, from anyone who has been out on it. Tick whatever is true — warning signs such as whumpfing or shooting cracks, the snow surface (powder, crust, icy) or the trail (busy, quiet, a skin track in) — and share it. That, with where and when you saw it, is the whole report. You see everyone else's; they see yours. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Sign in with a verified email address, then open the triangle button on the right of the map. The panel lists your own reports and offers "Report an observation". The "Show on the map" switch at its foot draws the community's reports; it is the same setting as the "Field observations" row in the layers menu. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The report takes your position from your device where it can, and you can nudge the pin before sending. If there is no GPS fix, move the map to set the spot yourself. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Unlike your pins and routes, reports are shared. They appear to other users on the community-reports overlay and on the Observations page — without your name, but with the place and time you recorded. Only report what you are content to make public. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A report made with no connection is held on your device and sent as soon as you have one, which is the point — the ground you are reporting on rarely has signal. {% endblocktrans %}
