{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk accounts are passwordless by default: enter your email address and follow the link that's sent to you — no password to remember. You can optionally set a password, or register a passkey on your device, from the account setup page. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} An account holds the things you save: the places and regions you pin, the routes you upload, the trips you plan, and the map areas you download to read offline. All of it is private to you, and everything except a field observation stays that way. {% endblocktrans %}

{% url "accounts:settings" as settings_url %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk can tell you when the avalanche bulletin changes for a place you have saved: a push notification on a device you allow, with email as the fallback (or always, if you prefer). Alerts are off until you turn them on in your account settings. Apart from alerts, the only email Snowdesk sends is a sign-in link, a password reset, or a confirmation that you changed your address. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} To hear about a new bulletin, subscribe a feed reader to the RSS feed for the country you follow: snowdesk.info/ch/feed.rss for Switzerland, and the same address with at, it or fr for Austria, Italy and France. The feeds need no account and list each bulletin as it is published. {% endblocktrans %}
