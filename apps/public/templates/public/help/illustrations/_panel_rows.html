One template serves all four panels by iterating the ``rows`` list on ``panel``, rather than four near-identical templates differing only in their literals; the literals live in apps.public.component_previews, where they are translatable. The component library's equivalent stand-in is public/partials/_ugc_panel_demo_rows.html, which is download-flavoured and hard-coded. It is left alone: it demonstrates the row's own slots (value, disclosure, action cluster) for a designer, which is a different job from showing a help reader what one panel holds. {% endcomment %}
