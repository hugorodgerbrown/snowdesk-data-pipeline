{% blocktrans trimmed %} The map colours every region by its avalanche danger level for the date being shown, from pale green (Low) through to red (Very High). Tap a region to select it: its name and that day's rating appear in the header above the map, along with a link to the full bulletin. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The magnifier searches regions and resorts by name and jumps straight to one. The crosshair centres the map on where you are, if you let your browser share your position. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The buttons down the right-hand side are the map's controls: layers, bulletin shading strength, offline downloads, your favourites, your field observations and your routes. The chevron at the foot folds them away when you want a clear map, and the "?" above it replays a short tour that names every one of them. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The (i) button in the bottom-left corner opens the legend: the danger scale, what each kind of pin means, the slope-angle bands when that layer is on, and the attribution for the map and the avalanche data. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Your saved favourite pins can be drawn on the map alongside the resorts layer — see "Favourites" below. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A community-reports overlay shows anonymised, publicly-shared field observations from other Snowdesk users on the map. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The field-observations button lets you submit conditions you have seen in person — see "Field observations" below. {% endblocktrans %}
