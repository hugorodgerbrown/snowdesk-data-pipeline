{% blocktrans trimmed %} Favourites let you pin specific spots — a resort, a trailhead, a favourite line — directly on the map. Sign in, open the star button on the right of the map, and use "Add a favourite": the map moves under a fixed pin, so you place it with one hand. You can also save a resort straight from its pin's popup. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} That same panel lists everything you have saved. Rename a pin in place, remove one, and use the "Display on the map" switch at the foot to draw them all — the switch lives here rather than in the layers menu. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Every favourite also has its own page you can bookmark or share — open it from "My favourites" or from "Your favourites here" on that favourite's region bulletin page. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Favourites are private: they are yours alone and are never shown to other users. A pin dropped with no connection is saved on the device and sent when you are back online. {% endblocktrans %}
