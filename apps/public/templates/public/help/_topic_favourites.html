{% blocktrans trimmed %} Favourites let you pin specific spots — a trailhead, a car park, a favourite line — directly on the map. Sign in, open the star button on the right of the map, and use "Add a favourite": the map moves under a fixed pin, so you place it with one hand. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} That same panel lists the pins you have dropped. Rename a pin in place, remove one, and use the "Show on the map" switch at the foot to draw them all — it is the same setting as the Favourites row in the layers menu, so turning one moves the other. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} To pin a resort, open it and press the star in its header. A pinned resort turns blue on the map, and search lists it under "Pinned resorts" before you type anything, beside the regions you have pinned. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The sun next to "Today" above the map opens Today: each region you have pinned, and each region a pinned resort is in, with today's avalanche danger and how it moved since yesterday, and your resorts listed under their region with today's weather. Tap a row to open that place. A red dot on the sun means a new alert. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Every favourite also has its own page you can bookmark or share — open it from "My favourites" or from "Your favourites here" on that favourite's region bulletin page. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Favourites are private: they are yours alone and are never shown to other users. A pin dropped with no connection is saved on the device and sent when you are back online. {% endblocktrans %}
