{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk has two maps. Conditions shows the mountains on a day: avalanche danger, the weather and what people have seen. It shows today unless you pick another date. Terrain shows the mountains themselves: how steep they are, where the tours go and where wildlife needs space. Use it to plan. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Switch with the toggle by the date, or with the tabs at the top of the layers menu. The menu lists the map's own layers first, in a tinted block, then the ones both maps share: resorts, regions, the basemap and user data, field observations included. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Each map keeps its layers as you set them. Turning one on or off changes that layer alone, and neither map switches anything on for you. In Terrain a tap on a region does nothing, because regions are about the day's conditions. Tap a resort or a pin instead: its sheet shows one Today row with the avalanche level and the weather. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} In Terrain the date shrinks to a calendar icon. Tap it to go back to Conditions on the day you left. The Today row in a resort's or a pin's sheet goes to Conditions on today instead, with the sheet still open. Picking a region from search also goes to Conditions on today. The map remembers which one you were on, and a link you share opens on the same one. {% endblocktrans %}
