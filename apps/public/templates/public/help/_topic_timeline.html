{% blocktrans trimmed %} The scrubber along the bottom of the map moves it through the season, a day at a time. Drag the thumb, and every region's colour changes to its danger rating for the date you land on. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The buttons either side of the track work like a media player: skip to the start or the end of the season, or play the whole thing as a timelapse, forwards or backwards. A second press stops it. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The date sits in a pill above the track, and everything else on the map follows it — the danger colours, the region readout in the header, and the weather symbols when that layer is on. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The weather layer is the one thing that does not follow the whole season. It is a forecast, so it covers today and the week ahead: scrub back past today and the symbols go, while the danger colours stay. {% endblocktrans %}
