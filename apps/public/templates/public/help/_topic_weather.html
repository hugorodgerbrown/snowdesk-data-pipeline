Content partial for the help page; included via the body_template parameter of includes/_collapsible_panel.html. Covers the SERVER-RENDERED weather — the per-location blocks on a resort page, the section on a saved pin's card, and the forecast page both link to. Not the bulletin masthead: SNOW-784 removed the Open-Meteo row there, because a bulletin is regional and a point forecast is not. The map's Weather overlay is a different surface with different rules and has its own topic below (_topic_weather_layer.html). Rewritten for SNOW-761 rather than restored. The pre-SNOW-762 version of this topic described a coloured band whose hue and icon shifted between a day and a night palette; that band is gone, so a reader would have gone looking for something that is not there. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Resort and saved-pin pages carry the day's weather: a condition icon and label, the high and low temperature, any new snow, the freezing level, wind, and when the sun rises and sets. It comes from Open-Meteo and describes one place. A bulletin covers a whole region rather than a place, so it carries no Open-Meteo reading — its weather is the forecaster's own description, further down the bulletin page under "Snowpack & Weather". {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A resort page shows one block per place we hold for it — typically the village, a mid-mountain station and the top — each labelled with its height. That is the point of showing more than one: rain in the village and snow on the summit are the same forecast read at two altitudes, and the difference is usually what decides the day. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Each of those places links to its own forecast page, and that is where the week lives: one column per day with its icon, temperatures, wind and freezing level. The first few days also carry an hour-by-hour breakdown and the later ones do not, because the forecast is not published at that resolution that far out. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Past dates show no weather at all. We record what the forecast said on each day going forward, so there is nothing on file for days before we started keeping it. A bulletin page for last winter shows the bulletin and no weather panel, rather than guessing. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The icon takes its day or night form from the time you are reading at, matched against sunrise and sunset where the place actually is — so a page read at midday looks like daytime whichever date you have navigated to. {% endblocktrans %}
