{% blocktrans trimmed %} The Weather row under "Conditions" in the layers menu puts a condition symbol on the map at every place Snowdesk holds — resort villages, mid-stations and summits, plus a point at the centre of each avalanche region. Under each symbol is the day's high temperature and the height that reading is for. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The height is there because the temperature means little without it. Two degrees at 1500 m and two degrees at 3000 m describe different weeks, and on a map that shows both at once the number alone would be misleading. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Zoomed out, places close together collapse to one symbol, and it is always the lowest of them. A valley reading is the one you can place, and its freezing level is what tells you whether it is raining where you would park. Zoom in and the higher stations separate out again. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The overlay is a forecast, so it covers today and the week ahead and nothing before that. Scrubbing the timeline back past today leaves the map with no weather symbols; scrubbing forward changes them without fetching anything, because the whole week arrives in one go. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Only curated places appear here. Your own saved pins are never added to this layer, even for you — their weather lives on the pin's own card, where nobody else can reach it. {% endblocktrans %}
