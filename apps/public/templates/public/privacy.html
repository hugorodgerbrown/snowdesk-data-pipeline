{% trans "The short version:" %} {% blocktrans trimmed %} You can read every bulletin on Snowdesk without giving us anything. An account stores what you save to it — favourites, routes, observations — and we keep the technical record of how it was created for twelve months. Delete your account and all of it goes. We don't sell your data, track you across the web, or use advertising cookies. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "1. Who we are" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk is operated by Hugo Rodger-Brown, an individual based in London, UK. Snowdesk is a free service that displays avalanche bulletins published by the WSL Institute for Snow and Avalanche Research (SLF) for Switzerland, by ALBINA / EUREGIO for Tyrol, South Tyrol and Trentino, and by Météo-France for the French mountain ranges. There are no employees; all operations are carried out by the sole operator. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Questions about this policy? Email: privacy@snowdesk.info {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2. What data we collect" %}

{% trans "2a. Your account" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} You can read every bulletin on Snowdesk without an account. If you create one, we collect your email address, and a password if you set one. You may also register a passkey, in which case your device keeps the private key and we store only the public credential — we never see the biometric or PIN that unlocks it. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2b. Things you save to your account" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} An account stores what you choose to put in it: favourite places, which are map pins with coordinates; ski-touring routes you upload as GPX files, which describe where a route goes; regions you subscribe to for email notifications; offline map areas you download; and any field observations you submit. We store these because they are the feature — none of it is collected in the background, and deleting an item deletes the record. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2c. Location" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The map can use your browser's geolocation to centre on where you are. That position is used in the browser and is not sent to us. Location does reach us in three other ways, each because you asked for something that needs it: a field observation carries the GPS fix taken when you tapped "Report"; a favourite carries the coordinates of the pin you placed; and an uploaded route carries its track. We also derive an approximate location — country, region and city — from your IP address at a few specific moments, described in section 2e. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2d. Field observation reports" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} When you submit a field observation, we ask your browser for a GPS fix. Your latitude, longitude and optional accuracy radius are stored with the report so we can match it to an avalanche warning region. Location is captured only at the moment you tap "Report" — we never track your position in the background or between sessions. Reports appear publicly as anonymous aggregate counts (for example "3 people reported whumpfing"); your identity and exact coordinates are never shown. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2e. Request records" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} At a few specific moments — creating an account, signing in, subscribing to a region, and opening a shared link — we store a record of the request. It holds your IP address, an approximate country, region and city derived from it, your browser's user agent and language, the page requested, and your session identifier. We use it to understand how people find Snowdesk and to investigate abuse. It is not written on every page view, and the approximate location comes from your IP address rather than from your device's GPS. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2f. Session data" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We use cookies that are strictly necessary for the site to work — see section 8. They carry no personal information beyond keeping you signed in. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3. Why we use your data" %}

{% trans "We process your data under the following legal bases (UK GDPR):" %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Email address and password — contract. We need them to give you an account and to sign you in. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Bulletin notification emails — consent. You gave it when you subscribed to a region, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Favourites, routes, downloaded areas and passkeys — contract. These are the features you asked for; we cannot provide them without storing what you saved. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Field observations — consent. Submitting one is a deliberate act, and you can ask us to remove a report you made. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Push notifications — consent. Your browser asks you before a subscription is created, and turning notifications off removes it. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Request records and session data — legitimate interest. We need them to keep the service secure, to investigate abuse, and to understand how people find Snowdesk. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Anonymous app-usage telemetry — consent. It is off unless you switch it on, and section 5 describes what it contains. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "4. How long we keep your data" %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Your account and everything in it — kept until you delete it. Deleting your account removes the account, your subscriptions, your saved items and the request records described in section 2e, in one operation and without delay. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Request records — twelve months, then deleted automatically, whether or not they were ever linked to an account. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Field observations — kept as part of the community record. Ask us and we will remove yours. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "Geolocation from your browser — never stored; used in the browser only." %}
  • {% trans "Server error logs — 14 days." %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} An unsubscribe removes the subscription; it does not delete your account. Delete the account itself from your account settings if you want everything gone. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "5. Who we share your data with" %}

{% trans "We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with anyone." %}

{% trans "We use a small number of trusted service providers to operate Snowdesk:" %}

  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Render (render.com) — hosts the Snowdesk servers. Your IP address and request data pass through their infrastructure. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Resend (resend.com) — sends your bulletin notification emails. They receive your email address for this purpose only. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — resolves the elevation of a location and supplies its mountain weather forecast. Requests are made by our servers for places, not for people; no personal data is shared with them. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} SLF / WSL — provides the avalanche bulletin data for Switzerland. No personal data is shared with them. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} ALBINA / EUREGIO (avalanche.report) — provides the avalanche bulletin data for Tyrol, South Tyrol and Trentino. No personal data is shared with them. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} Météo-France (meteofrance.com) — provides the avalanche bulletin data (BRA) for the French mountain ranges. No personal data is shared with them. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans trimmed %} PostHog (posthog.com) — processes the anonymous app-usage events described below, if you have telemetry switched on. We never send bulletin content, your email address, or your IP address as part of these events. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "8. Cookies" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk uses two strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie, and a CSRF token that protects forms from cross-site submission. Neither carries personal information beyond keeping you signed in, and because both are strictly necessary no consent banner is required under UK PECR. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party cookies. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Snowdesk also stores things in your browser that are not cookies and are never sent to us on their own: your light or dark theme choice, the map areas you download for offline use, and the queue our anonymous usage-data telemetry (section 5) uses when you have it switched on. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. {% endblocktrans %}
