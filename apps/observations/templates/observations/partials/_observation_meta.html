Place first, age second: the user is looking at a list of their OWN reports on a map, and the region is what tells two of them apart. " · " on the first line — no "reported" verb — and, since SNOW-883, the report's three word address beneath it when there is one. THE SECOND LINE IS THIS SURFACE ONLY, and the divergence from the map's pin popup (map.js's activateCommunityReport) is deliberate rather than an oversight. Those two lines used to match exactly, so a report read the same whichever way the user reached it, and that symmetry is still worth keeping where it can be. It cannot be kept here. This list is scoped by ``for_user``, so a reader is looking at their own reports; the map overlay is public and carries everyone's, which is why ``apps.public.api.community_reports_geojson`` rounds its coordinates to three decimals (~80-110 m) and never sends the raw field. A three word address names a 3m square, so putting one in that payload would undo the anonymisation that endpoint exists to apply. See ``apps.observations.views._attach_three_word_addresses``. The age is relative ("2 hours ago"), not an absolute stamp ("11 Aug 2026, 05:29") — the reader should not have to do the arithmetic. ``naturaltime`` is Django's own, so the words are translated for every locale rather than by us. A relative age is only true when it is rendered, and this one is rendered on the server. The list reloads from observations:list on every panel open, so it is right whenever the panel is opened WITH SIGNAL — and that is exactly the condition a bulletin app cannot assume. Away from signal the endpoint is network-only (sw.js), nothing re-renders, and a row read four hours later would still claim "1 hour ago". Hence the `` element: the instant is in `datetime`, and static/js/relative_time.js recomputes the text from it on a tick and, more to the point, whenever the page comes back into view after a phone has been in a pocket. That arithmetic needs no network. The markup sits INSIDE the blocktrans rather than the sentence being assembled from two halves around it — a locale is free to put the age before the place, and only a whole-sentence msgid lets it.
