Rendered by mcp-auth's consent POST in place of a 302 (``MCP_AUTH["CONSENT_RETURN"] = "page"``). The site CSP's ``form-action 'self'`` covers the redirects that follow a form submission, so a 302 from the POST to the app's callback would be blocked; a meta refresh is an ordinary navigation and is not. The link is the fallback for a browser that ignores the refresh. mcp-auth sets ``no-store`` and ``no-referrer`` on the response; the referrer meta repeats the second for the refresh itself. ``target`` is the app's registered callback plus ``code``/``error`` and ``state``, checked by mcp-auth against the allowlisted patterns.
