Rendered by apps.favourites.views.region_pin_list and swapped by static/js/map_region_panel.js into the pinned-regions section of the region + date panel — the surface the #region-readout chip discloses. WHY IT IS HERE AND NOT IN THE FAVOURITES SHEET. A region pin was a row in the pins sheet from SNOW-802 until SNOW-814, beside dropped pins and saved resorts, and it never fitted: it has no coordinate for the map layer to draw, no name of the user's own to rename, and no forecast to expand — so that sheet's every other affordance was absent from it, and its "Display on the map" switch could not include it. The chip that names the selected region is where a list of regions belongs: the pin control is the star roundel in the same header (public/partials/_map_region_pin_control.html), so pinning and finding what you pinned are one surface, and the chip stops being a dead control when nothing is selected. Rows are the shared UGC row (includes/_ugc_panel_row.html), not a shape of this panel's own. Two slots, and both differ from the pin row's because a region is not a place: the name frames the REGION — ``focus_region`` rather than ``focus_target``, since a region carries no coordinate and its polygon is already on the map (SNOW-811 set that precedent for the downloads panel's region rows); the action is a filled star, not a trash. Unpinning removes the user's pin, not the region — see _region_pin_row_actions.html. NO META LINE, and that is the shape of this list. It carried today's rating and a dated bulletin link for a day, and both were wrong HERE: the panel above them follows the scrubber (``snowdesk:date-changed`` repaints the summary), while these rows are fetched once and stamped with the date of the fetch. A user who scrubbed to April read "Open bulletin for 8 Apr 2026" in the summary and "No bulletin available for 4 Sep 2026" in every row beneath it — one live date and one dead one, a few pixels apart. Making it live was the alternative and it is not worth the machinery: the rows would refetch on every scrub, to show a rating for a date the user is passing through, which is not what a list of pinned regions is for. Hugo: "If it's not 'live' then we should remove it completely — which would simplify things. It's then just navigation." A row answers one question, take me there; the region it takes you to answers the rest, in the panel above, for whichever date is showing.
