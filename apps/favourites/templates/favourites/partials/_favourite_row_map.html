This row is includes/_ugc_panel_row.html instead — the shape Hugo's design gives all three UGC panels: label, muted meta line, icon actions. Rename and Remove are the actions, both icon controls (_favourite_row_map_actions.html), pencil then trash. Rename is this panel's only extra: the pencil, or a click on the label, puts the label into an inline edit. There is no link on the primary line. It WAS one for a day — the row's title linking to the favourite's own detail page, replacing an even earlier "Details →" control beside it — and Hugo's design drops both: the label's click is the rename now, and a favourite's detail is reached by tapping its pin on the map, which is where the user already is. The row's id is what the Remove form's hx-target addresses, and it matches _favourite.html's own ``favourite-`` so the two variants stay addressable the same way. Meta line: the pin's region when one matched, else its coordinates. A pin can fall outside every known boundary (region is nullable), and an unnamed pin's coordinates are the only thing distinguishing it from another — an empty secondary line would leave rows uneven and say nothing. No `value` slot: that column is for measured quantities (downloads' size) and a favourite has none. Leaving it out is the point — a column that means "size" on one panel and something else on another means nothing.
