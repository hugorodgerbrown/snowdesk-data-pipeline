That file is NOT forked or changed: the account manage page renders it and keeps its always-visible rename field, which is right for a page whose whole purpose is managing favourites and wrong for a list glanced at on the map. This row is includes/_ugc_panel_row.html instead — the shape Hugo's design gives all three UGC panels: label, muted meta line, icon actions. Rename and Remove are the actions, both icon controls (_favourite_row_map_actions.html), pencil then trash. Rename is this panel's only extra: the pencil puts the label into an inline edit. The primary line does nothing at all. It has been three things in three days — a link to the favourite's detail page, a "Details →" control beside it, and a click-to-rename target — and it is now inert on every panel: a favourite's detail is reached by tapping its pin on the map, where the user already is, and renaming is the pencil's job (see includes/_ugc_panel_row.html for why the label lost that job). The row's id is what the Remove form's hx-target addresses, and it matches _favourite.html's own ``favourite-`` so the two variants stay addressable the same way. Meta line: when the pin was saved, prefixed with its region when one matched — "Alpstein · saved 3 Feb 2026", Hugo's own design for this row. It used to be the region, falling back to the pin's coordinates. Two problems with that. The coordinates are the wrong reading: a pair of four-decimal numbers is precise, unmemorable and impossible to place, so the line said least exactly where it was the only thing on offer (an unnamed pin outside every known boundary). And the date was missing altogether, though it is the one fact that orders a list of saved places and tells a user which of two similar pins is the recent one. Region is nullable — a pin can fall outside every known boundary — so the date alone is the default and the joined form exists only for the case that has both. Same shape, and the same date format, as the observation row (observations/partials/_observation.html), which had the pairing right first. No `value` slot: that column is for measured quantities (downloads' size) and a favourite has none. Leaving it out is the point — a column that means "size" on one panel and something else on another means nothing.
