Rendered by apps.favourites.views.places and swapped into the favourites sheet's [data-favourites-rows] by static/js/favourites.js when the Saved tab opens it (window.pwaFavouritesSheet.open({ list: "places" })). The roundel's Favourites panel loads favourites:list instead and is unchanged. Built by apps.favourites.today.build_places: Today's list (favourites/partials/_today.html) with the user's dropped pins folded in. One group per region the user pinned, holds a pinned resort in or dropped a pin in, in name order, never by urgency (Hugo, 2 Nov 2026): * the region's own row — its name and today's EAWS level, a link that opens the region sheet on the map (map_panel_trail.js, as Today's rows do), and a "…" menu of Share and Delete only when the region itself is pinned. A region listed only because a resort or a pin of the user's sits in it has nothing of its own to share or delete, so no menu. Its Delete empties the region's own row only (``place-region-head-``), never the group, so the resorts and pins under it stay until the list is read again; * the pinned resorts in it, each with today's weather, a link to the resort sheet and the same Share / Delete menu; * the dropped pins in it, through the one saved-pin row (favourites/partials/_favourite.html) — Share, Rename, Delete as on the Favourites panel — with the pin's height on the meta line. The pins in no region come last, under "Elsewhere". No observation counts (SNOW-1479, "Not changing"). The root names the back pill a followed row leaves on the sheet it opens (``data-panel-key="places:now"``), which reopens this list. Context: places : Places — from build_places.
