THE SHAPE IS DECIDED BY HOW MANY CONTROLS THE ROW HAS. In a restricted area — a map overlay, a sheet, a panel row — one CTA is a bare icon and two or more collapse into a "…" menu. So: * a REGION PIN has Remove and nothing else (SNOW-802: its label is the region's name, so there is nothing of the user's to rename, and it has no coordinate to share) — one control, rendered as a bare trash; * a PLACED PIN has Rename and Remove, plus Share when its location has a three word address — two or three, rendered as a menu. That rule replaced SNOW-658's "a visible trash on every row, one tap not two", which SNOW-830 had already narrowed to exclude the routes row.
