Both showed the same thing — favourite_detail.html includes the very same _favourite_card.html the card endpoint renders — and a GET wearing a button is the wrong element for it. SNOW-658 collapsed them into a single progressively-enhanced link: href navigates to the real page without JS, hx-get renders the card into the panel below with it. The hx-get is load-bearing beyond convenience — it is what drives the SNOW-418 offline cache write-through in favourites_offline.js, which fires on htmx:afterOnLoad and would never see a plain full-page navigation.
