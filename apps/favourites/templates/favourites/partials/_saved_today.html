The top of the Favourites panel: one compact row per saved place, every kind the user keeps (a dropped pin, a saved resort, a region pin), each with the region's danger today, how it moved since yesterday and the forecast it borrows from the nearest curated point. Built by apps.favourites.saved_today.build_saved_today, most urgent first. An inset block inside the panel's "Places" section rather than a section of its own, so the panel keeps the one-label shape the four UGC panels share (tests/public/test_ugc_panels.py). The rows below it are still the dropped pins, with their rename / share / remove menus; these are a reading of the day, and carry no actions. What a row's name does depends on what the place is: a pin or resort frames it on the map — the ``data-row-focus`` button static/js/row_focus.js serves for every panel row; a region pin is a real link to the region's bulletin, carrying ``data-panel-open="region"`` so on the map static/js/map_panel_trail.js opens the region's sheet instead (favourites.js closes this panel for it). The danger tile is the EAWS square (``danger-tile`` + ``data-level``), the trend the house chevron turned up or down, a level plus a word so neither colour nor shape carries it alone.
