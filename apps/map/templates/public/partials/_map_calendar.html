{% load i18n %} {% comment %} public/partials/_map_calendar.html — the map's date picker (SNOW-792): a month grid that opens from the bottom-left date control and commits a chosen day through the season scrubber's own commit point. The scrubber track is a few hundred pixels wide and spans twelve months, so a pixel is well under a day and hitting a chosen date by drag is a fiddle — and below 640px the scrubber does not render at all, which makes this the only date control there. It owns no commit path of its own: picking a day dispatches ``snowdesk:scrub-to`` and map_scrubber.js does the rest. SNOW-794 MOVED THIS SURFACE. It used to be a ``position: fixed`` sheet in the bottom-RIGHT slot, sharing geometry with the favourites, observations and routes panels, on the reasoning that every panel over the map should appear in one place. Its trigger then moved to the bottom-left row, merged into the date readout — and a panel opening on the far side of the map from the control that opened it is a worse rule than the one it was obeying. So it now behaves like its neighbour instead: an absolutely-positioned card inside .map-legend, opening UPWARD from the row with an 8px gap and left-aligned to it, exactly as #map-legend-card does. That also means it is no longer handed to ``window.pwaOverlayBounds.positionSheet`` — it is not a fixed sheet with a floor to find, it is anchored to the row's own top edge, which moves with the row. (The bottom-right sheets still use that module.) Height-bounded and scrolling, like every surface that floats over the map: a six-row grid fits easily today, but the anchor is low and the card opens into the viewport with nothing else to stop it. Included by _map_embed.html INSIDE #map-legend, as a sibling of the legend card and the row's buttons — it is ``position: absolute`` and needs that row as its containing block. Context expected: * season_start — datetime.date: first day of the highlighted season. * season_end — datetime.date: last day of it. * today — datetime.date: the today marker, and the latest selectable day — the future is the one hard stop. * calendar_month_names — 12 localised month names, January first. * calendar_weekday_abbrs — 7 localised weekday abbreviations, Monday first. The season bounds are a HIGHLIGHT, not a limit. The map carries weather as well as bulletins, so a day in September has something to show even though no bulletin covers it; an earlier draft made the season the selectable range and could not reach those days at all. The grid itself is built by static/js/map_calendar.js rather than server-rendered: a season alone is ~245 day buttons and the reachable range is longer still, so putting them in the home page's DOM for a control most visits never open is a cost every visitor pays for a few who don't. What the server DOES supply is every translated word — the month names below travel as a data attribute and the weekday header is rendered here — so no user-facing string is a JavaScript literal (see docs/i18n.md). {% endcomment %} {# Card treatment matches #map-legend-card, its sibling in this row — the #} {# positioning lives in .map-calendar (static/css/map.css) rather than in #} {# utilities here, because it needs the same bounded-height calc the legend #} {# card uses and that cannot be expressed as a class string. #}
