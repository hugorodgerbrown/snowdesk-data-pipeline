{% load i18n snowdesk_time weather_icons %} {% comment %} public/partials/_place_sheet_today_row.html — the place sheet's one row in Terrain (SNOW-1422, behind ``map_modes``). Terrain has no day of its own, so "Today" is the only day it can name (Hugo, 5 Nov 2026, option B of the mockup). The row stands in for every section of the place sheet marked ``data-dated``: the region's level tile, "Today", and under it the level's name and one weather reading — the day's condition icon and high. It is ``data-terrain-only``, so map.css shows it in Terrain alone, and in Terrain ``map_place_sheet.js`` asks for today's body, so the row is always today's. A tap on it (``data-place-sheet-today``) switches the map to Conditions on today with the sheet left open, and the sheet repaints in full. A visually hidden "Show today's conditions" ends its accessible name, so a screen reader hears where the tap goes. Rendered by ``_place_sheet_body.html`` inside the peek, for a resort, a saved pin and the reader's location alike, so a phone shows it without a drag. Context: day_rating — the region's RegionDayRating for the day, or None. level_label — the level's name, or "". weather — ``place_sheet._today_row_weather``'s reading (``icon_filename``, ``condition_label``, ``temp_max``), or None. day — the day the body was read for. another_day — whether that is not today; then the row names the day. {% endcomment %}
