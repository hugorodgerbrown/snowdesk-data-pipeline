{% load i18n l10n %} {% comment %} public/partials/_map_compass.html — the map's compass (SNOW-1449). Included by _map_embed.html directly under the zoom roundel at the top of the bottom-left stack, and by the component library at /_components/?slug=map-compass. A small roundel, the zoom and help roundels' size, always shown — north-up or turned, flat or in 3-D, for every reader. The needle points at north, so it turns against the map's bearing; a press turns the map back to north (static/js/map_compass.js, which keeps the pitch). The glyph is a north needle: its north half filled, its south half drawn in outline. It is not includes/_icon_compass.html, which is the wind mark. Context (optional; on /map/ the script sets it from the map): * compass_needle — number; the needle's turn in degrees, the map's bearing negated. Unset, the needle points up, as /map/ boots north-up. {% endcomment %}
