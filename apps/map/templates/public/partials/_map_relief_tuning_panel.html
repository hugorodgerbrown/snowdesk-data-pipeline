The winter map past the Swiss border paints its own relief, a hillshade and a grey rock tone on steep ground (static/js/alpine_relief_core.js). This panel lets a staff reader try other strengths on their own map: where the rock starts (the slope, 25–60°), how strong the rock tone, the shadow and the highlight are (0–100 % of full). Moving a slider repaints the relief (window.pwaBeyondSwitzerland.tune) and keeps the values on this device (localStorage); Reset goes back to TUNING_DEFAULTS. Every other reader always gets the defaults. See docs/decisions/the-winter-map-beyond-switzerland-is-restyled-osm.md ("Tuning (staff)").
