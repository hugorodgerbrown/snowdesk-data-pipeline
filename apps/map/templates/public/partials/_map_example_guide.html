The welcome card's "Explore an example route" goes to routes:example, which puts the example share in the session and lands here with ?route_share=&example=1&d=. The route opens as any followed share does; this partial is the guide over it, and static/js/map_example_guide.js runs it. Four steps — say what just happened to the date, drag the point the guide has placed along the profile, zoom in ("Show me" does it) until the line shows the slope, read the colours — then a finish card, and the map back on today. Nothing is locked (Hugo, SNOW-1230): close the route or wander off and the bar folds into the paused pill, which keeps its step; change the date and the pill offers the way back to the example's day. Exit and Back to today always put the map back on today and say so. "Keep exploring this example" (SNOW-1244) ends the guide but keeps the route and its day, with the pill holding the way back to today. Everything a reader sees is rendered here so {% trans %} reaches it. The JS fills in only the day ([data-example-day], formatted in the reader's locale), the route's name ([data-example-name]), the step count and the zoom bar.
