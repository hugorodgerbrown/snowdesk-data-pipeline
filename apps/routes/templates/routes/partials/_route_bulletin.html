THE TWO EMPTY CASES ARE DIFFERENT CLAIMS, and ``readings_for_track`` returns ``[]`` for both, which is why the view passes ``sampled`` separately. A route nothing has sampled has not been looked at; a sampled route with no readings has been looked at and crosses no ground we hold a forecast boundary for. Rendering either as "your line meets nothing" would be a statement about avalanche terrain that this page has not earned. Nothing here is user-supplied — a region's name is curated and the day is the server's own — with one exception that is not on this surface: the route's NAME is written into the panel by map.js with ``textContent``.
