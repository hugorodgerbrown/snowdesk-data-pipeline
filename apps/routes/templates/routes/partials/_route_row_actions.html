TWO CLUSTERS, AND WHICH ONE RENDERS IS DECIDED BY OWNERSHIP (SNOW-764). An OWNED row gets Share, Rename, Remove — Share only when ``sharing_enabled`` says so, which is a surface check and nothing else: which listing asked, and therefore whether a handler is wired for the click (see apps/routes/views.py's route_list). Trash stays last, on the design's rule that the destructive control is in the same place on every panel; Share goes FIRST rather than beside the trash, because it is the only one of the three that involves anybody else and putting it next to Remove would seat "give this away" beside "destroy this". A PENDING row — one the visitor was sent and has not claimed — gets a single Save. It is not an icon: the other three name actions a user already understands from every other panel, while this one is the whole point of the row and the only thing on the surface asking to be pressed. A 17px glyph for the row's primary action would be a puzzle. The two never both render. A pending row's route is not the viewer's, so Rename and Remove have nothing to address — and they are owner-scoped server-side anyway, so a rendered pencil would be a control that 404s.
