META LINE — the two figures a route is read by: its length, and how much climbing it carries. "12.4 km · 850 m ascent". ``ascent_m`` is nullable and the null is MEANINGFUL: a GPX with no data anywhere yields null, and Route's own docstring is explicit that "we don't know" and "flat" are different facts — rendering the second for the first would be a safety-relevant lie about a route someone may be planning to ski. So the joined form exists only for a route that has an ascent figure, and a route without one shows its distance alone rather than "0 m ascent". Tested by tests/routes/test_views.py::TestRouteList::test_a_null_ascent_is_omitted. No `value` slot.
