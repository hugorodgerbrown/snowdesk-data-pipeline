{% load i18n %} {% comment %} routes/partials/_route.html — one imported-route row. The ONE route row: every surface that lists a route renders this file (SNOW-711). Rendered directly by apps.routes.views.route_create/route_rename after a route is ingested or renamed, and looped over by both list variants (_route_list.html and _route_list_map.html). WHAT THIS REPLACED. SNOW-685 shipped this row as a bordered box holding an always-visible name field and an underlined "Remove"; SNOW-686 gave the map sheet a SECOND row built from the shared UGC row, because that was the shape Hugo's design gives every UGC panel. The two then rendered the same route two ways. SNOW-711 kept the shared row and deleted the fork (_route_row_map.html), the same move it made for favourites — and unlike that pair, these two had no slot that genuinely differed: a route has no detail page, so neither surface has a disclosure. * a row is not a card. It draws a hairline and nothing else (includes/_ugc_panel_row.html), so a list reads as a list; * rename is the pencil, and happens IN PLACE on the label; * Remove is the trash, last in the row, in the same place on every list a user meets. The primary line is inert, as it is on every panel: renaming is the pencil's job, and a route has no detail page to link to. The row's id is ``route-``, which is what its own Remove form addresses. META LINE — the three figures a route is read by: its length, how much climbing it carries, and how much dropping. "12.4 km · 850 m ascent · 1100 m descent". Ascent and descent are both here because they are not each other's mirror: an out-and-back climbs and drops the same height, a one-way traverse does not, and the pair is what separates them at a glance. They are never netted — see Route.descent_m. ``ascent_m`` and ``descent_m`` are nullable and the null is MEANINGFUL: a GPX with no data anywhere yields null, and Route's own docstring is explicit that "we don't know" and "flat" are different facts — rendering the second for the first would be a safety-relevant lie about a route someone may be planning to ski. So a route with no vertical data shows its distance alone rather than "0 m ascent". Tested by tests/routes/test_views.py::TestRouteList::test_a_null_ascent_is_omitted. The two are normally known or unknown together (one elevation series produces both), and the common cases are the first and last branches below. The two single-figure branches are not dead: a legacy row backfilled by routes migration 0002 takes its descent from the SIMPLIFIED points while its ascent came from the full-resolution track, so a track mixing elevation-bearing and elevation-free points could in principle lose every elevation vertex to simplification and end up with one figure and not the other. Each branch is a whole phrase rather than assembled fragments, so a locale can order it freely. No `value` slot. That column is for a single measured quantity (downloads' size), and a route has two figures that belong together in one line — splitting one of them into a right-aligned mono column would make the column mean "size" on one panel and "distance" on another. Same reasoning as the favourite row's own note.
