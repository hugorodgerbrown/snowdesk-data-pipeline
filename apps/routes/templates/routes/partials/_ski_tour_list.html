Rendered by apps.routes.views.ski_tour_list into #ski-tours-sheet, the object sheet static/js/ski_tours.js opens. A TAP NEVER PICKS A TOUR ON ITS OWN: more than one tour in reach lists them here, which near a hut is nearly always. Two shapes, one row: * ``rows`` — "6 routes pass here": every tour within reach of the tap, each row named by its summit (the handle a reader chooses by) and sorted by it. * ``summit`` with ``up_rows`` / ``down_rows`` — every tour to one summit or hut, the ways up and the ways down apart, each row named by SAC's own name for the way ("Von der Silvrettahütte SAC"). A way down is drawn dashed in its swatch, as on the map. Each row is a button carrying ``data-ski-tour``; ski_tours.js opens the tour's panel on a press. The figures are SAC's: its grade, its height gain (↑, or ↓ for a way down) and its guide time. Vocabulary (Hugo, 28 Oct 2026): titles say ROUTE, SAC lines included; "tour" only in a sentence. Context: rows — list of row dicts (apps.routes.views._tour_rows), or summit — apps.routes.services.ski_tours.Summit, with count — how many tours go to it, up_rows — its ways up, and down_rows — its ways down. {% endcomment %}
