WHY THESE ARE IN A MENU AT ALL. Four inline controls (Share, "Plan a trip", Rename, Remove) on a 380px map panel row left the route's name rendering as "Mont Fort – Ba…", and the name is the one thing a user scans a list of routes by. Collapsing them behind one trigger gives the name the row. The other three UGC panels keep their visible trash — see includes/_overflow_menu.html's header for why the ruling is narrowed to routes rather than reversed. EVERY HOOK IS THE ONE IT ALREADY WAS. `data-route-share`, `data-row-rename`, `data-route-rename`, `data-row-remove` and `data-route-plan-trip` are unchanged, and so are the five modules that read them: static/js/routes.js, inline_rename.js, row_rename_commit.js and row_removed.js each resolve by upward `closest()` traversal or a row-scoped `querySelector`, never by depth or sibling position, and every listener is delegated on the sheet or on document. Moving a control inside a menu inside the same `
  • ` is therefore invisible to all of them. ORDER. Plan a trip, Share, then — after a rule — Rename and Delete. The first two are the actions that involve somebody else, which is the pairing _route_row_actions.html already argued for when Share sat first in the inline cluster; the rule separates "do something with this route" from "change or destroy this row". Delete stays last, on the design's rule that the destructive control is in the same place on every panel. Remove is the only coloured item. A menu of four neutral lines gives a user nothing to aim at or avoid, and the one irreversible action is the one worth marking; it takes the `status-error` pair the inline trash took on hover. The inline trash was neutral at rest on the argument that a row of red bins reads as an error state — that argument does not carry here, because a menu shows one Remove at a time and only while it is open. It says "Remove", not "Delete", although the design's handover names the item Delete: the control's accessible name is already the translated "Remove " that every other panel's trash carries, and WCAG 2.5.3 (Label in Name) requires the visible label to appear in it. Changing one of the pair alone breaks voice control; changing both would fork this row away from the wording the other three panels use for the same action. WHAT KEEPS ITS ELEMENT.
