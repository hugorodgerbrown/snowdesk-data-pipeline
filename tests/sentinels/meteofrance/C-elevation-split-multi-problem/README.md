## Why this is variant C

`RISQUE1=1` (low) below 2400 m, `RISQUE2=2` (moderate) above 2400 m — two danger
ratings with an elevation split.  `SAT1=4` (wet_snow), `SAT2=2` (wind_slab) —
two distinct avalanche problem types.  The combination of elevation split and
multi-problem is the defining characteristic of variant C for MF bulletins.

Note: the original scope picked Chablais on 2026-05-21 as variant A (single-level),
but verification against the research dataset showed that this date has an elevation
split.  The plan revised the picks: Belledonne (massif-008) is the clean single-level
A, Aravis (massif-002) is the clean elevation-split B, and Chablais (massif-001) on
2026-05-17 is C.
