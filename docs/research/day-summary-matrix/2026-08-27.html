Rules for editing a cell

  • One sentence, ending in a full stop. Tests assert both.
  • Never govern a verb from %(problems)s. It holds one name or four, so “wind slab is named” breaks on the second. Use a noun phrase: “with %(problems)s at the surface”.
  • A cell may use %(problems)s and %(transition)s, nothing else. The level words belong to the transition clause.
  • Quiet cells must not name problems; every other cell must. Both directions are tested.
  • No two cells share a sentence — a duplicate means one combination was written twice and another missed.
  • Don’t claim an arrival. “with wind slab arriving” is false when wind slab ran all morning. Say what is in play; let the movement carry the direction.
