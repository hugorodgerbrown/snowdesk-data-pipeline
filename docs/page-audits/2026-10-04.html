Consequences

What this week's shape changes about older findings

The homepage was the answer to a question no ticket asked. The /compare/ finding was written as "needs a footer link". The remedy that landed is a link from the front door, a better place than the footer, and a reminder that the decision in test_compare_page.py (never in the footer) and the audit's complaint were compatible all along. The same move is open to the explainers: the homepage already has a three-card "where to read more" row.

A last-reviewed date can now mislead. The docs sweep refreshed bulletin-guide.md without adding the two providers it lacks, so the staleness signal this series used to track the gap has gone quiet while the gap has not.

SNOW-1062 and the homepage disagree. One decided danger is not a property of a route; the other still invites a visitor to see a route "against the day's danger".
