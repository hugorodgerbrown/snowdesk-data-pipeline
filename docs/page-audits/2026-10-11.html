Consequences

What this fortnight changes

The homepage answers "where should discovery live?" by default

With a real front door and three outbound cards, the explainers and the Claude connector are now absent by choice of omission rather than for lack of a landing page. Whoever owns the homepage copy is now the owner of both open reachability questions.

A review stamp can hide an unfixed finding

The docs-alignment sweep bumped the bulletin guide's date, so a staleness check on last-reviewed no longer flags it. The finding is now only visible by reading the content, which is exactly what this audit does and a linter will not.

3. Do SNOW-679 — it is Ready for dev, the guide covers one provider of three, and its date now says otherwise.

4. Correct the footer comment that says nothing links to /compare/.

5. Comment on SNOW-937 whether the pause is deliberate.
