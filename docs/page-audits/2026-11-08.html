Consequences

What this week changes

Widening the alerts flag needs three copy edits, not one

Last week's advice was to add an alerts topic to Help. The Help accounts topic must also lose its "sends no bulletin alerts" sentence, and the privacy policy's wording is already live. All three change together or the pages disagree.

The tab bar gives Help a second front door and Compare none

In the installed app, Help is a top-level tab and also reachable from the "?" menu. A surface the app now treats as primary still has no route from the homepage to the explainers, so the unlinked pages are further from users in the app than on the web.

A flagged feature is promoted on the homepage

The 3D card and 3D-branded hero show to flag holders only, so nothing public is wrong, but when map_3d opens to every signed-in reader the homepage, Help and the offline copy all need a 3D explanation that does not exist.

Recommendation

What I'd do next

1. Reconcile alert copy before the flag widens: Help accounts topic, a new alerts topic, and the privacy wording.

2. Link the explainers and the Claude connector from the homepage, footer or Help. Unchanged for two audits.

3. Decide the fate of /compare/: relink or drop from the sitemap.

4. Give 3D a Help topic ahead of map_3d opening to signed-in readers.

5. Replace the SLF-only worked examples in the bulletin guide.
