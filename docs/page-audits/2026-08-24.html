Two account pages shipped Done, reachable by nobody

zero inbound links

SNOW-677 built /account/observations/ and SNOW-713 built /account/routes/ — both marked Done, both routed under accounts:observations / accounts:routes, both fully rendered and covered by tests. Neither URL name, and neither literal path, appears in any template or script anywhere in the tree outside their own view/test files. The nav dropdown (templates/includes/nav.html) carries only two <a> tags for the whole account area — My account and Settings — and hub.html shows only Subscriptions and Favourites. No footer, map surface, or partial links to either page.

The nav template itself carries a comment, added by SNOW-705, describing the intended state: "Each account section is its own page, reached from here: Settings today, and Favourites, Routes, Observations and Subscriptions as SNOW-668, SNOW-677 and their successors split them out of the hub. Adding one is adding one <a> to this list." The two pages exist; the two <a> tags don't.

No open ticket currently owns fixing this. SNOW-668 ("Build /account/favourites/") is the ticket whose description commits to "adding one <a> to the subscriber menu" — but its own scope is the Favourites entry, and it is Todo, unstarted. It separately describes "the target menu, once every section has landed" as five items including Routes and Observations, but nothing in its scope, or anywhere else, is chartered to add those two links now that their pages already exist.

Consequences

Two things this week's work changes about the older tickets

Connections between this cycle's decisions and tickets written before them — visible from neither side alone.

Both of Snowdesk's flagged differentiators are now shipped by AvalancheClarity

docs/competitors.md's Feature inspiration list named two unclaimed ideas: push-on-new-bulletin alerts (item 1) and cross-provider consolidation (item 2). The 08-19 scan found item 1 shipped. This week's 08-23 scan found item 2 has shipped too — and specifically across France, Switzerland and pass-through coverage for Austria/Italy, which is all three of Snowdesk's own provider geographies. SNOW-7 (send the daily email) remains Todo, unassigned to a cycle. The gap this creates is no longer "we haven't built the differentiator yet" — it is "the differentiator has been fully claimed while we were building something else," which changes what re-prioritising SNOW-7 is actually weighing against.

SNOW-668 needs a scope note before anyone picks it up

SNOW-668 is titled around building /account/favourites/, and its own description promises "one <a> to the subscriber menu" — singular. But two more pages (SNOW-677, SNOW-713) have since shipped Done with the same gap, and nothing currently charters closing it for them. Whoever picks up SNOW-668 needs to either land all three nav entries in one pass, or split the observations/routes links into a ticket of their own — otherwise the next audit finds the same orphaned-page problem a third time, on whatever ships next into the same gap.
